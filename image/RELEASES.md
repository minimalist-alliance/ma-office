## Baked into 0.3.2: faster wakes (TD-227, `sub/alliance-wake`)

*Not a released row.* A wake of an Alliance desk (`t4g.medium`, stopped after 20 idle minutes, started as the office opens) took a median 27.9 s from the office loading to the first Browser frame and 31.1 s to Chromium's tabs, on `ma-desk-0.3.1` (5 wakes). With these two changes: 24.0 s and 24.2 s (5 wakes each; `docs/benchmarks/2026-11-alliance-desk.md`, which also has the stretches EC2 and the app own). README, "Faster wakes"; runtime spec §3.2.
- **cloud-init is off after a desk's first boot:** `recipe/wake/ma-off-after-first-boot.conf` (a drop-in on `cloud-final.service`; its `ExecStartPost` writes `/etc/cloud/cloud-init.disabled` after a good final stage) and `recipe/wake/20-ma-dhcp.network` (a MAC-free DHCP fallback, because netplan's file for the desk names the card's MAC and is no longer re-rendered). Installed by `build.sh`, not from `files/`, so `selfhost/install.sh` doesn't copy them to a member's own machine. The kernel to `deskd`'s hello: median 11.7 s to 8.3 s. **The image must never carry the marker:** `build.sh` removes it before its assertions and asserts it absent.
- **`desk-browser-prewarm.service`** (`files/`, enabled by `build.sh`): starts `desk-xorg`, `desk-audio` and `desk-browser` once `deskd` answers, on a desk with an age band, not a Guild Desk, no `/data/desk/no-prewarm` (the kill switch) and a passing `desk-browser-start --check`. Chromium's tabs after the app's `connect` answer: median 5.9 s to 2.4 s. Started at multi-user.target instead, it made `deskd`'s hello 2.1 s later (5 wakes), which is why it waits.
- **`verify.sh` gains section 8,** 6 checks (`verify-wake.sh`: static, first, later, fallback, boot, gates), **so the next bake must pass 6 more checks than it would without them** (61 and 3 `INFO` on 0.3.1; 0.3.2 also has `desk-batch`'s 8). `verify.sh` sets the kill switch (`/data/desk/no-prewarm`) right after the box is up, so no other section sees a Browser it didn't start, and section 8 takes it off. Each part was seen to fail with what it guards broken (`evidence/alliance-wake-2026-11-09/mutations.txt`). **`verify.sh` as a whole has not run with them; the 6 parts have, one at a time, on a `t4g.medium` test desk running 0.3.1 with these files installed by hand.**
- **Tried, not kept:** a `docker.service` drop-in without `After=network-online.target` (no gain, 5 wakes), the Browser started at multi-user.target (hello 2.1 s later), EC2 hibernation (works; resume is not faster at gp3's default throughput, the public IP changes and the link takes minutes to come back).
- **Not proven:** the next bake itself (nothing was baked); a wake with a real `Officer` desk's 24-hour idle; cloud-init off on a desk that has been through an `ma-update` root replacement in production; the owner's desk is not on this image.

- **Self-hosted desks** (`selfhost/install.sh` copies the recipe's files but not `build.sh`'s block): the narrower origins reach them too, so they stop taking Debian's stable point releases automatically and keep its security updates, daily, by Debian's own timers, which nothing masks there.
