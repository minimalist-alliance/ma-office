Maia in Motion

Version 7

Your device asks for less motion, so she stays almost still here.

Six moods, all at once

One small score per reply drives her for 20 to 30 seconds, then loops. Her eyes stay open while she is awake. Everything here runs live at 30 fps.

A chat, live

She thinks, speaks while the text streams, plays the score, falls asleep and wakes. Her bubble is 64 px in the chat, next to the same Maia at 200 px.

    While a desk stream plays
    
      
    She drops to 15 fps to leave the machine to the desk. She moves about as far, but no single frame steps more than 1.5 px at 56 px, and beats run longer. Same score on both.
