# Vision

Minimalist Alliance is a safe, calm place to do your digital life from anywhere, on any machine. Personal and work things live side by side but stay apart. Compartmentalization, low stress and ease of use come first.

## The office

Each member has a private office, opened in a browser: one open canvas of desks that you move across, zooming into the desk you want to focus on. A serious member can run it full screen and treat it as their only operating system.

- Every member starts with a Personal desk, a Work desk and a Guild desk, and can add more.
- Each desk is a private cloud machine that belongs to one member. Nothing on it is shared unless its member shares it.
- Desks hold small, focused desk apps. Apps can be made bigger or smaller, like apps and widgets on a phone, and no app is required.

## The first apps

- **Notebook:** notes that are easy to write and format, found by tab or by day.
- **Browser:** a real browser that runs on the desk. By default it shows a simplified view of each page, with the real page always one switch away.
- **Chat:** channels, one per person or group, whatever the way a message travels.
- **Agent:** an assistant for each desk. It works only with what is on that desk, uses the member's own AI provider and limits, and always shows which provider it is.

## Guilds

Members can form guilds to share what they choose between their desks.

## Principles

- **Privacy first.** A desk is its member's alone.
- **Less noise.** Fewer words, fewer decisions, in the product and around it.
- **Secure sign-in.** An authenticator app or a trusted identity provider.
