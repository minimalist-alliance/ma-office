# Draft ADR: Desk Maia, Maia Mode's Maia on the member's home desk

**Status:** Proposed (draft), Nov 14 2026, built on `sub/mm-maia-runtime` for Maia Mode Phase 1; her key changed to the company account the same day (decision 11, `sub/mm-maia-company`). Owners: the Desk Engineer (desk-agent, deskd) and the AI Engineer (her prompts, the note-taker, the to-do reader, the evals). **Dark by default**, the owner's alone first.

**Reads with:** `CONCEPT.md` Part 2 (Maia Mode's design: §1 where she runs, §3 the list, §4 coordination, §5 notes, §6 to-dos, §7 the team view, §8 the safety model, §10 cost); the draft ADR [the Agent's tools and approvals](draft-agent-tools-approvals.md) (§3 approvals, §6 taint, §12 Courier, §13 To-do, §14 messages between desks, §15 auto-approve, §16 an agent's access); deskd [`API.md` §23](../../services/deskd/docs/API.md) (the contract) and §18, §22; ma-platform `docs/maia.md` (Support Maia, "Account help": §1 to §9, §9 the switchboard, §16 memory, §17 teens), which this ADR doesn't change.

## Context

The owner, Nov 12: Maia Mode turns the office into a quiet room with Maia in it; she "drives all of the work", coordinating the agents on the member's desks and "being very careful not to bring too much to the user's attention at one time". Phase 0 built what she needs from the desks: approvals and durable runs, the Notebook, To-do and Courier tools, messages between desks, and auto-approve. On Nov 14 the owner asked for Maia Mode "ASAP on my desk", and the concept's four questions took their defaults:

1. She runs on their home desk. (She first ran on the member's own key; since Nov 14 she runs on Minimalist Alliance's AI service: decision 11.)
2. She acts without a tap only inside narrow per-desk grants.
3. Officer and Founder tiers, later; the owner's alone first.
4. The office stays open in Phase 1 (a background tab is enough). Phase 2 (Nov 15, the owner approved: "Yeah she's kind of useless if she can't keep going") lifts it: decision 12.

Two Maias exist after this. Support Maia ("Account help") never reads desk content. Desk Maia works with the member's desks, and since Nov 14 her model calls go through Minimalist Alliance's AI service (decision 11): what she reads and writes passes through it to the model provider, under MA's zero-training terms, and is neither kept nor logged there; everything else of hers (her thread, notes, grants, the list) stays on the member's desks.

## Decisions

### 1. She is an agent on the home desk, with a fixed id
- Desk Maia is a desk-agent agent row with the id **`maia`** (her product name, as the owner's rule on ids asks), role `staff`, made when the member turns Maia Mode on. Her thread, limits (ADR 0043), approvals (§3) and audit are every agent's; her binding is the desk's own, the company service (decision 11), and her memory has Support Maia's rules.
- **She isn't a team agent.** The team's lists, its cap, the lead's tools and proposals, `message_agent` links and the Agent app's `agent.list` and `agent.state` don't include her; `agent.update`, `agent.delete` and every team method answer `not_found` for her. deskd's own view (the heartbeat) does see her runs, so a desk doesn't sleep under her.
- **No agent may message her,** on this desk or another: no link can name her, and `agent.desks.answer` refuses her as an answering agent. Her briefs go one hop out and nothing comes back but answers.

### 2. Dark by default, listed desks only, and a kill switch
- Nothing of hers runs until the member turns Maia Mode on from their own session (a token with input), with the disclosure's version (`maia-mode-v2` since Nov 14; decision 11). The Platform's flag (`client-config` `maiaMode`, the Platform builder's) decides who sees the switch at all.
- **A desk must be listed.** The Platform's list (`/v1/me` `maiaMode.enabled`) doesn't reach a desk, so the desk's own switch lists it: desk-agent's `/data/agent/maia-switch` saying `on` (or `DESK_AGENT_MAIA=on`). With neither, Desk Maia is refused (`not_listed`; the Platform read's MMP-L1). Phase 1's release writes `on` on the owner's desk alone.
- **The kill switch** is the same file saying `off` (or anything else, or not a plain file), or `DESK_AGENT_MAIA=off`: read at every call, `off` winning, and an unreadable switch is off. It stops her messages, every Maia mutation, her briefs and the to-do runs, and her runs within 10 minutes; reading her settings, turning it off, revoking a grant, a to-do switch's off and deleting her history still work.
- **Her chats are kept 90 days.** desk-agent's upkeep (every 10 minutes and at start) deletes her messages older than that with their approvals, runs, steps, results and marks, her reply extras and her note log; held notes go at 7 days. The disclosure says so.
- **Turning it off** revokes every grant at once (desk-agent's and deskd's record) and stops her runs. Notes and to-dos stay: they're the member's.
- **Teens** never have it (desk-agent and deskd refuse the Agent for them already); a Free tier and a Guild Desk refuse turning it on (deskd refuses a Free desk by its own tier record, before desk-agent hears).

### 3. The list, its urgency and its interruptions are code
`maia/Attention` is one pure function over what the office observed on the member's desks (`agent.maia.observe`, kept in RAM only), her own briefs and notes, and her spend. At most 3 items and at most one Now; urgency only by the concept's fixed rules (an approval expiring within the hour, a task stopped by a limit, a failed desk, her spend at 80% of her cap, a to-do due within 2 hours by its due time); one item per desk and kind, with a count; new Next items batched every 30 minutes or when the member is back; nothing new while the member types; an interrupt at most once every 15 minutes, only for a new Now, never with a sound. Words are templates; a desk's text is plain, cleaned of hidden and bidi characters and cut to 60 characters. **She has no tool that makes, raises or hides an item**, and nothing a desk writes can.

The concept put the list in the browser; it's on the home desk instead, so the rules are one tested piece of code that her tools and every device read the same way. The office still only sends structure (ids, states, times, counts, names), never page or message text.

### 4. Her briefs: `delegate` under a grant
- A brief is W6's `desk_message` from agent `maia` (§14): the member's send half on the home desk and their Allow on the target desk, which picks the agent that answers.
- **Her grant** per desk adds `scopes` (`brief`, `show`), `keepsToItself` and an expiry (7 days by default, 30 at most), made only by the member (`agent.maia.grant.set`, a session with input; **no step-up**, per the owner's Nov 7 rule and Security's ATA Q3). desk-agent keeps it, and **deskd keeps its own record** (`agent/MaiaRecord`, HMAC'd, recorded from the member's relayed call cut to desk-agent's answer, failing closed), which is what counts.
- **The class.** Without a live `brief` grant her brief is refused `maia_grant` before anyone is asked, by desk-agent's guard and by deskd. Under one it is `own` (the concept's `delegate`) even when her run holds private data, since it stays within the member's own private desks. It rises to `send` (a card) when her run read a private answer from another desk that **keeps to itself**, or from a desk she has no grant for any more. deskd knows the desks a run read from by its own record of the answers it gave the run and by desk-agent's `D:<deskId>` flags, which only ever add. A private answer taken in her wait is kept on her reply, so her later runs carry its desk too.
- **Her private briefs** (Security's DMA-M1). A brief from a run holding `S` is `private` in the sealed envelope (hers only; the receiving deskd ignores the word from any other agent). The answering run on the other desk starts with `S` as well as `U`, from desk-agent and from deskd's own record, so its browse actions are `egress` there. Its answer back to her desk stays `own`, because the private data came from that desk, unless a tool on the answering desk gave the run that desk's private data: then it asks, as W6 does (DMA-L5). This needs the release on every desk she briefs: an older desk drops the word.
- **Caps:** `briefsPerDay` (the tier's: Officer 60, Founder 120), W6's 20 a grant a day, and, only if the member sets one, a day cap on her in credits (`coordinatorDailyCredits`, against her calls' `costCredits` on the desk; Nov 15, replacing the retired `coordinatorDailyUsd`). At 100% she stops briefing and her next turn ends with one plain line until the desk's midnight. Her Maia allowance on the company service, in credits, is otherwise her only limit (decision 11).

- **The member's wallet** (Nov 15, the owner: "People aren't going to want to use and trust Maia Mode if it just destroys their wallets", and "Will users have to approve that EVERY time?"). Before a brief goes, code (`maia/Wallet`) estimates it from the answering agent's usual task (its desk's own ledger, through the office's read of that desk's team view) times the brief's size, and holds it only when it would pass that agent's own limit (`maia_limit`, never waived), take her briefs past the member's daily budget for them ($3 by default), or cost more than 3 times that agent's usual task and more than the member's ask line ($0.50) (`maia_held`). Held, code posts its own card in her thread with the estimate and Go, "Always OK for tasks like this", Cheaper way and Skip (at a limit: Cheaper way, Try anyway, Skip; with nothing left, Another way and Skip). Inside the budget nothing asks. The budget, the ask line and "Always OK" change only from the member's own session or their tap on code's card; she has no tool for any of them, and the desks' hard caps stay as they were (defence in depth). API.md §23, "Her briefs and the member's wallet".

### 5. Notes come from a clean taker and code files them
After each member message a **note-taker** model call (her service's `notes` task, no tools, its rules as the desk's context) reads **only the member's own messages** in her thread, never her replies, answers, results or files, so it never holds untrusted content. Code checks its JSON (at most 5, each one plain line of at most 280 characters, the memory's secret and instruction refusals), applies the member's own word rules, and files each area's notes as **one `notebook_write` by her code** in a clean run that read nothing, in the member's "From Maia" tab, on that day's page for the area, **done without asking** under her access's Notebook switch for that tab (§15). The gate's new `admitUnasked` never puts a card in front of the member: a call that would ask isn't made. The approval row is the note's record and its Undo; her reply carries the chip ("Noted for Work · Undo"). An `unsure` note is held and asked about once when the conversation goes quiet, in a code-written message with choices.

**Phase 1 files every note on the home desk** (a page per area). The concept files Work notes on the Work desk; that needs a structured note envelope that the target desk's code files without a model, which is next (question 1).

### 6. To-dos from Courier: a quarantined reader and a code filer
For each agent the member switches it on for (Courier and To-do on in its access), every 30 minutes and at start: `courier_new` through deskd (known contacts only; Requests, spam and bulk left out), then **one model call with no tools** that answers only `[{messageId, action, due, confidence}]`, then **code** checks each (the id in the batch, one plain line of at most 120 characters with nothing shaped like a link, an address or Markdown, a real date, confidence 0.5, the desk's day cap) and calls `todo_add` with `source: {kind: "courier", ref: {messageId}}`, which deskd checks the run read and labels from Courier's contact record. Dedupe is To-do's own. The cursor moves only for a run that ended `done`. Real email waits for Courier's email transport.

### 7. Choices, and the member's weight for them
Her `ask_member` tool puts 2 to 4 choices on her reply (code checks: short, plain, distinct, no markup; "Other" always). A tap is `agent.maia.choose`, which sends the label as the member's message. The weight (0 to 1, from 0.8) moves by taps, "Other", passing by and asks for more or fewer read in code; it's kept on the home desk as **counts only**, so every device sees the same one, and the member can reset it. Her prompt gets the weight and the concept's rule.

### 8. What she never does
The concept's §8.1 "Never" column holds because **no tool exists for it**: approve; see or handle a key; reach another person, account or desk outside the member's own; delete for good; buy, upgrade or pay; change her own limits, tools or grants; call `/v1/agent/*` or `/v1/admin/*`; act for someone under 18. Her model is offered `office_desks`, `office_desk_status`, `office_show`, `ask_member`, the home desk's To-do tools, `desk_message`, `plan_update` and her memory: no Files, Browser, Notebook, Courier, `follow_up`, workspace, proposals or `message_agent`. A show names `(deskId, app enum, ref id)`, never a URL.

### 9. The team view
`agent.usage.summary` on every desk: per agent per day, spend, calls and tokens (the ledger), tasks done, failed and stopped (replies' ends), approvals asked, approved, declined and undone; each agent's headroom (what's left of its day and month limits, what a brief from another desk may spend there, its usual task); what her briefs cost on that desk today and this month; and on the home desk her briefs' spend across the desks with the day's budget. Counts and dollars only (the dollars are the member's own agents on their own keys; Maia's own usage isn't in it); the office sums the desks; nothing goes to the Platform.

### 10. Audit
The home desk's hash-chained log, ids only: `maia.on`, `maia.off`, `maia.set`, `maia.grant`, `maia.revoke`, `maia.brief`, `maia.note`, `maia.note.undo`, `maia.show`, `maia.todos`, `maia.choices.reset`, `maia.forget`, beside every call's own `tool.call` and `approval.*` rows.

### 11. Her model: the company account (Nov 14)
The owner, Nov 14 about 9:30 pm MT: **"Maia ALWAYS runs on the company account."** And: "Maia Mode will be tuned based on company tested models and optimizations and so we need control over which model and provider is used to give the best experience." (ma-platform ADR 0140, contract `docs/contracts/desk-maia.md`.)
- **Members never add a key for her;** their own desk agents keep their own keys. Her binding is the desk's own: `KeyRing.COMPANY` (`ma-maia`, no key material, lent as an empty lease, in no key list), provider `ma` (`ProviderId.MA`, in no provider list, no key accepted for it), task `turn`. It's set when her row is made and at every start (desks from before are rebound); `agent.bind` for her is `forbidden` `company`; the gateway refuses `ma` to any other agent and anything but `ma` to her.
- **The desk sends the task and the context, never a model.** `MaiaServiceProvider` sends each call as one `maia_model` tcall to deskd (desk-agent can't reach the Platform), with the task (`turn`, `step`, `notes`), the desk's dynamic context and the thread; files only as a line naming them. Her Maia Mode instructions moved to the Platform (`MaiaProfile.PROMPT` is gone): her service puts them first, byte-stable for its cache, then the desk's context, and picks the provider, the model, the effort and every optimization from its settings.
- **deskd relays it as the desk role** (`MaiaModel`, `DeskMaiaClient`): `POST {endpoints.maia}/v1/desk-maia/call` with a fresh `X-MA-Desk-Auth` per call that also signs the audience (`x-ma-maia-id`) and the exact body's hash (`x-ma-body-sha256`); deskd's own facts refuse first (not `maia`, a Guild Desk, not an adult, Free, no service, a malformed or oversized body, more than 4 at once). The Platform checks the member again (listed, adult, paid tier) and meters the call against the member's Maia allowance and the $100 pool.
- **Allowance and spend:** a `429 limit` ends her run with one plain line (the room says when it resets, in the member's own time) and nothing more is sent until then; nothing was spent. **Since Nov 15 (the owner: members never see dollar amounts for Maia; contract 0.4.8):** her calls are the company's, so the desk keeps no dollar figure of hers and none of the member's own-key limits counts or binds her; each answer's `costCredits` is counted on the desk for the member's own cap, and the room shows her credits (what's left of the month's, a renewal date, where she's drawing from, the lighter model), never money and never a countdown. Day and week amounts pace only; past the month's credits and top-ups she keeps working on a lighter model and says so.
- **The disclosure is version 2** (`maia-mode-v2`): "What goes to an AI provider" now says she runs on Minimalist Alliance's AI service within the member's Maia allowance, passing through it to the model provider, neither kept nor logged nor used for training, while desk agents keep the member's own keys. A desk that holds only v1 refuses her sends and turns (`precondition_failed` `disclosure`) until the member accepts v2.
- **Her memory goes to MA's account with every run,** so it keeps Support Maia's rules (ma-platform `docs/maia.md` §16): how the member likes answers, how she can help, what they've set up, never anything private; code refuses private-shaped lines (the Platform's check ported, `memory/MaiaMemory`), and older lines that fail it are never pinned into her runs.

### 12. She keeps going while the member is away (Maia Mode Phase 2, Nov 15)
The owner, Nov 15 about 2 am MT: "Yeah she's kind of useless if she can't keep going." His phone app will be Maia Mode only. ma-platform ADR 0141 (which amends ADR 0016) holds the Platform's half; this is the desks' half.
- **Her coordination lives on the home desk, as durable code around durable runs** (`maia/Away`, `AwayStore`): no office is needed. Her briefs are kept (`maia_briefs`: the envelope, the desk, what she expects back); the answers come to a durable queue (`maia_reports`); her check-ins (`maia_check_in {minutes}`, 5 to 1,440) start her run at their time, at a start if missed by under a day; mail-to-to-dos run as before on each desk while it's awake. **Presence:** the member is away when no office has said anything for 2 minutes (`agent.maia.observe`).
- **Briefs say what she expects back** (`desk_message`'s `expect {fields ≤ 6, then: report | think, urgentIf, newWork}`), and code adds a fixed block: the answering agent writes its full answer as its reply and ends with one `REPORT {status, urgency, importance, summary, fields}` line. **On the answering desk, code keeps the full reply as a write-up** (`desk_writeups`, 90 days, the answering agent's own data on its own desk) and sends back only a short report, at most 1,500 characters, with a pointer (`writeup=wu-…`, `Reports.compact`).
- **Reports are read by code first.** One that needs nothing from her (done, her brief said `report`, every field she asked for filled, not `now` or `critical`) is filed by code: the list's result and the day's summary, no model call. The rest wait, due by the agent's own urgency and importance: `now` or `critical` at once (3 s, so reports that come together are read together), `soon` within 2 minutes, `later` when 3 pile up or after `reports.laterMaxMin` (30 by default, 5 to 240, `agent.maia.set`). When the first is due, every waiting report goes to one run of hers (origin `{kind: "maia", role: "reports"}`), and while she's in a run they wait for it to end. **Agent-set scores are untrusted (`U`):** one desk agent's `now`/`critical` past 3 an hour counts as `soon`/`normal`, and they decide only when she reads, never what reaches the member (decision 3's rules stay code's). **Nothing waits forever or vanishes:** a row stays until a run of hers finished reading it; a batch a stop cut off waits again at the next start; a failed batch is tried again in 5 minutes, at most 3 times, then counts as read.
- **Her reader** (`maia_read {desk, writeup?, query?, question}`, class `read`, `U`+`S`): her code asks the other desk's code (a W6 message marked `[maia-read v1]`, never an agent run there; refused by code if it can't be answered) for write-ups its agents left for her briefs, **only those whose answer needed no card there** (the closing `desk_answer` ran without that desk's private data); then one small call on her service (task `read`, Haiku by default, on MA's account, counted in her credits) pulls out only what answers her question (at most 1,200 characters). Under her `brief` grant, never past "keeps to itself", with her run's taint, and the desk's origin added to her run. **The routing rule, in code and in her instructions:** reading or extracting from what exists goes to the reader; new work, the desk's apps or tools, the agent's own context, browsing or changes go to a brief. A brief naming a write-up is refused `use_maia_read` unless it says `expect.newWork`.
- **Keeping desks awake only while needed:** `agent.state`'s `maiaAway {on, inFlight, wakeAt, away, asks, done}` tells deskd whether she has work in flight (her run, briefs out in the last 2 hours, reports waiting, a check-in within 20 minutes) for the Platform's bounded hold, and her next check-in for its wake. Her briefs may ask the Platform to wake their desk (`wake` on the outbox row, her `msg`s only); the Platform decides (the member's wake list, hours, 12 a day). **The Platform's word comes back** (deskd's `desk.maia.away {away, hold}`, Security's MA-M1): while the member is away and it says not now (the kill switch, the member's switch, today's hours used), nothing of hers starts and nothing holds the desk; what's due waits for the member. Check-in runs are capped at 24 a day.
- **While the member is away:** her questions (`ask_member` on a reply while away) and her finishing (nothing out, nothing waiting) are counted for deskd, which rings the bell (and an email if the member allows) through the Platform; approvals waiting on any desk too. When the member is back, **"While you were away"** greets them: code's words from the log (`maia_away_log`, ids and template lines, 30 days), with her open question moved onto it as choices, or code's own "Tell me more" / "That's all for now" (`choices.kind: "away"`; "That's all" starts no run).
- **deskd's carrier** (`agent/MaiaAway`): only while the Platform's reply says `maia.away`; what the office hasn't carried in 30 s goes to the Platform as it is (`desk.desks.outbox`, `desk.desks.take {olderThanMs}`); what waits there is opened and checked as the office's deliver does and handed to desk-agent in order, stopping at a transient failure; outcomes go to `desk.desks.done`. Never on a Guild Desk; envelopes stay sealed end to end.
- **Her to-dos while away** (`sub/mm-away-fixes`, the staging proof): the concept lets her file to-dos and notes in "From Maia" without a tap, while away too. A run of hers the member didn't start has no member's message to name, and the report a to-do comes from came after her wait, so deskd had given that run no source and every such `todo_add` was refused `source_unknown`. Her runs now name the answers in her thread to deskd (`origin: {kind: "maia", answers}`); deskd gives the ones it opened for her, with `U` on its own copy of the run's taint, so `desk {}` is a source the run was given: the `todos` group, done without asking under her To-do switch, the item marked untrusted (ATA-L1 unchanged). Nothing else she can do while away changes, and her notes stay her clean taker's (decision 5).
- **No thinking goes back to her service** (`sub/mm-away-fixes`): it moves the note to the newest turn on each call, so every replayed thinking block followed a changed turn, which Opus 5.5's preserved thinking refuses with a 400 (`unavailable` `provider` on the desk; every step after a call in which she thought failed on staging). desk-agent sends back only the Responses wire's encrypted reasoning. Replaying hers again needs the service to keep each call's note in its turn.
- **Kill switches:** the desk's own `maia-switch` (decision 2) stops all of it; the Platform's `maia_away#current` (the console) and the member's own switch stop the away part; Maia Mode with the office open is untouched by those.
- **Audit:** the home desk's log as before, plus `maia.writeup` and `maia.read` on the answering desk (ids and counts).

### 13. What reaches the member: the focus shield (Nov 15)
The owner, Nov 15: Maia as "a protective focus shield", bringing what's close to the member's work first and holding the rest "like a really really good executive assistant"; and yes to the home desk seeing which desk and app the member is on and when they pause (ids only, never leaving their desks; the `/privacy` line is the Sales Engineer's). Design `~/ma-coord/design-drafts/maia-shield/CONCEPT.md`; API.md §23 "What reaches the member".
- **Code decides, on the home desk** (`maia/Focus`, `Reach`, `Deliver`, `Rhythm`, `Learn`, `Shield`): no model ranks or words anything here, so it costs no credits. Her model sees only a hold ("holding until …") and, in the turn that set it, the member's stated task.
- **Focus is structure only:** desk, app, item ids (a Files folder hashed in the browser), people as contact ids, typing and idle as edges, visibility, a device id, completed actions. Memory only; never to the Platform, Support Maia, a model or a push. An office that sends no `focus` gets the list as before.
- **The gate is the list's code-rule Now and the member's let-through list** (at most 5, identity checked: an email's DMARC pass, a text's known channel; "a desk stuck" always on, as the desk's failed state). No score, learning, agent status or desk text opens it.
- **Delivery:** offers at breaks by distance; interrupts with a heads-up, spaced 30 minutes (15 for a deadline that can't wait), riding up to 2 more on one card, deadline items just in time; catch-ups at the member's times; nothing while typing, on a call, idle or in the first 10 minutes of focus hours or a hold; Work and Personal by the clock; the deadline floor; nothing lost. The numbers are the evals' (`maia/eval/ShieldScenarioEvalTest`, 27 days, four arms).
- **Kept:** `maia_focus_*` rows in `agent.db` (settings with the member's own pending names, item states by key, learned rules, counts and misses for 90 days); "Delete Maia Mode history" clears all but the settings.
- **Seams:** Phase 2's briefs get `urgentIf` from the list; results carry the report's status (`needs_decision` and `blocked` say someone waits, never the gate); the documents work fills `desks[].docs` from each desk's `docs.feed {front: true}`; Courier's summary fills `desks[].courier` from deskd's `courier.attention` (structure only, no model); the welcome's Q4 to Q7 write `agent.maia.reach.set` (the office's `reachFromWelcome`), asked once the home desk answers `agent.maia.reach.get` (`SHIELD_READY` is true: the welcome and the shield ship in the same release).
- **The bell agrees with the shield** (the finish, `sub/mm-shield-2`): Phase 2's while-away questions and finishing ring through the shield's rules (`maiaAway.ring`: not in quiet hours, at most 12 a day), the shield's own interrupt-level items ring as kind `reach` once the Platform takes it, and approvals stay with each desk's deskd, so nothing rings twice. Calls (Commons voice) and screen shares (the office's Privacy shield) are "do not disturb": every card and catch-up waits for their end. Office tile badges are still, neutral counts while the shield is on (Courier's a dot), keeping the accent only where something may break through (`reach.accent`).

### 14. The decision tray: papers that need the member's decision (Nov 15)
The owner asked for "brief papers and supporting materials" to sign off, decline or follow up. Every held thing that needs the member's decision becomes a paper (deskd API.md §23 "The tray"), built by code on the home desk from what the shield holds: who's asking, a line, the materials, the answers that fit, and where the answer goes (set by code, never a model). Her part is one line and up to 3 answers on a report's paper, in the turn she already runs (`paper_note`, labels through a filter that drops links, addresses and long numbers): no extra credits. She has no tool to answer or approve; an Approve names the payload hash the member saw, and the office approves only that (Security's TRAY-M1); every answer is the member's tap, carried to the asking desk by the member's own device and token (an approval's payload hash checked first), or, for a report, sent from the home desk as her brief's code-made follow-up. Send, spend, delete and egress take a second tap on a strip that says what goes where. Approvals can't be dismissed; Later only while the deadline floor allows. The answer row is queued on the home desk until it's carried; the member's words are wiped once sent. Drafting replies to people's mail and texts is **not** built (the owner's answer, Nov 15: no): a person's paper offers Reply · Remind me · No reply needed and the member writes; the seam is the paper's `freeform.draft` (it would fill the box, never send). In its place the owner asked for **quick reply**: a reply arrow on every card, held row and paper about a person's message opens a field in place (a drag down or a long press as shortcuts), their last message quoted from Courier at draw time; a typed reply needs no second confirm but waits 5 s with Undo, then goes the way the message came, and only where that desk's Courier can send it.

## Consequences
