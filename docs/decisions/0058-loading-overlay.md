# 0058. The office's loading overlay

**Status:** Proposed (the owner's ask, approved 2026-11-12; the design and animation are the Product Designer's, `design-drafts/loading-overlay/`, Nov 12)
**Date:** 2026-11-12

## Context
- The owner, approved through the Lead on 2026-11-12: "While an office is being loaded for any tier we need an overlay with a loading animation combined with the logo that fades away when the office is actually ready." Every tier, band and theme, and the phone. Reduced motion gets a still.
- **Today's loader** (`web/CLAUDE.md`, "How it reaches a browser"): Platform's page, ma-platform `sites/desk-loader/index.html`, owns the HTML, `#ma-root` and a blocking `/_loader/theme.js` that paints the theme's ground (`html[data-theme]` and its background). `loader.js`, a module, then fetches client-config and `ma-release.json` and injects the bundle's CSS and entry. There is no loader of the office's own: until this, a member saw the bare ground, then the office building in pieces (the shell, the canvas from this browser's mirror, the desk list's answer and its refit, `/v1/me`'s cards, the free tier's chunk, fonts).
- **The real "ready" signals in code**, none of which existed as one signal:
  - the desk list: `useDeskStore` `status` leaves `loading` only on Platform's answer (`setDeskList`, `setDeskListError`, `setUnpaid` in `state/deskStore.ts`); the mirror (`ma.desks.v1`) paints desks while it is still `loading`.
  - who the member is: `useSession` `me` and `meFailed`. A returning visit fills `me` from the last visit's record first (`showLastKnown`, `meIsLastKnown` in `platform/session.ts`), which has no status, band or tier; only `loadMe`'s answer or failure is Platform's word. A closed account's `403 account_closed` sets neither, and is heard by `platform/accountClosed.ts` instead.
  - the tier's parts: a free member's chunk (`free/office.tsx`) is imported once `me` says `free`, and its `open` fills `useFree`.
  - fonts: `fontsReady()` (Inter and Jost, at most 800 ms) gates the stage's mount (`Shell.tsx`).
  - the canvas: Konva's layer fires `draw` on each frame it draws; `ma:first-canvas-paint` marks the first, which can come before the list answers.
  - the cards: `noDeskNotice` (`shell/deskNotice.ts`) picks the `DeskNotice` that stands in for desks; the sign-in, refusal, export and OpenRouter pages are `App.tsx`'s.
- The office's CSP (contract `hostnames.md`) is `default-src 'self'` with no `style-src`, and `script-src 'self' blob:`: no inline `<style>`, no `style=""`, no inline script.

## Decision
1. **The loader page draws it, from the first paint: the Designer's markup and CSS.**
   - `index.html` links `/_loader/loading.css` after `theme.js` (render-blocking, `'self'` under the CSP), gives `#ma-root` `aria-busy="true"`, and puts `<div id="ma-ld" role="status" hidden>` after it (`hidden`, so a page whose `loading.css` didn't load never shows it unstyled; the stylesheet's `[data-theme] #ma-ld { display: flex }` shows it): the kit's three marks inline, whole (`ma-mark-primary.svg`, `ma-mark-zen.svg`, `ma-mark-on-dark.svg`; only each root tag gains a class and `aria-hidden`; each file's own id prefix, so none is shared), a 2 px track with its glint, the 10 s line "Still loading your office…" (`aria-hidden`, since it's in the page from the start) and the screen readers' "Loading your office". Both lines are drafts for Sales.
   - `loading.css` is the Designer's `src/loading.css` with two changes, below. It shows the overlay only under `[data-theme]` (no script, no overlay: the `<noscript>` line shows), on `theme.js`'s own three grounds, one mark per theme by ADR 0066 (primary on Light, the Zen mark on Zen, on-dark on Dark), and moves only opacity and transform: nothing for 500 ms, then the mark and track appear (500 ms), the glint crosses every 1.8 s, the line fades in at 10 s; under 600 px the phone's sizes.
   - `web/CLAUDE.md`'s rule keeps the office's own mark in an `<img>`; the overlay is the loader page's, which the office doesn't render, and it carries each file once with its own prefix, so the brand README's warning about two copies on a page doesn't apply (the Designer's note).
2. **It takes no input, at any time.** `pointer-events: none` from the first paint, nothing focusable, no focus moved, the office not `inert`. A click or tap reaches the office under it; the keyboard was never the overlay's. This is the UI/UX brief's rule. *(Changed from the Designer's draft, which blocked the pointer until ready.)*
3. **"Ready" is the first frame of the office the member will use, holding still** (`web/src/chrome/loadingOverlay.ts`, `useOfficeReady` in `Shell.tsx`). The overlay starts to go on the first of:
   - **The office settled and drawn.** All of: `useDeskStore` `status` isn't `loading` (Platform's answer, not the mirror); `meAnswered` (`platform/session.ts`: `meFailed`, or `me` that isn't the last visit's record); for a free member, the free tier's chunk in (`useFree` `free`) or its import failed; fonts in (the stage mounted). Then the canvas's next `draw`, and one animation frame after it, so that frame is on screen. The arrival is an instant refit (`refit` in `WorldStage`'s effect on the answer), so there's no camera flight to wait for; a phone lands on its desk the same way.
   - **A card instead of desks:** any `DeskNotice` (the membership cards, the closed account's, waiting on a parent, no desks, can't reach), once fonts are in and the next frame drawn.
   - **Not an office:** the sign-in card, the refusal card, the export page once its chunk is in, OpenRouter's return window (`LoadingDone` in `App.tsx`). A trip to sign in or to `/account` leaves the page with the overlay still up.
   - **The office failing to render:** React's `onUncaughtError` (`main.tsx`) ends it, so it doesn't sit over a blank page; the error is reported as React does by default.

   **Not waited for:** the desk links connecting (a sleeping or waking desk is ready as the office shows it), a desk's video, WebRTC, the office background picture (ADR 0094 paints its colour first), desk style textures, Maia, and every other lazy chunk (the first load's apps work from the shell's first render).
4. **How it ends** (the Designer's steps; the same in `loader.js` and `loadingOverlay.ts`): on the same frame, `aria-hidden="true"` on `#ma-ld` and no `aria-busy` on `#ma-root`; then `ma-ld-out` (the mark, track and line in 280 ms, the ground in 480 ms from 120 ms, 600 ms in all), or `ma-ld-fast` (the bare ground in 200 ms) if the mark hadn't begun to show (`.ma-ld-in`'s opacity still 0); removed on its own `animationend`, or after 700 ms. A hidden tab, or an overlay `loading.css`'s safety already hid, is removed at once. `aria-hidden` says it's going, so whichever of the loader and the office comes second leaves it be. The office does it once a page and marks `ma:office-ready` with the reason (`desks`, `card`, `signIn`, `refused`, `export`, `connect`, `error`).
5. **Reduced motion is the Designer's still:** the loop's 1080 ms frame, the glint centred on the track; the mark and track appear by opacity alone (200 ms at 500 ms), the line by 200 ms at 10 s; the ending is a 200 ms linear opacity fade. *(The UI/UX brief said "removed at once"; the Designer's opacity-only fade is kept, as no motion is in it.)*
6. **The loader ends it itself when the office won't** (`sites/desk-loader/loader.js`):
   - **The kill switch:** client-config `loadingOverlay: {enabled: false}` removes it at once when client-config answers (absent is on). Platform's member function doesn't send it yet: it needs a settings row and a Management Console dial, as `openTiles` has. Until then the way back is redeploying the previous loader.
   - **A bundle from before this** (a rollback): only a release whose `ma-release.json` says `"loadingOverlay": 1` (written by `vite-plugins/release.ts`) keeps it; otherwise it's removed at once at the entry's `load`, and that office paints as it always did.
   - **A failure** (client-config, `ma-release.json` or the entry not loading): the ground fades (`ma-ld-fast`) to the loader's own line, "Desk is unavailable right now. Try again in a minute."
   - **The safety time, 15 s** after `loader.js` starts: the full fade, whatever the office is doing, so it can never trap anyone (the UI/UX brief's rule). A cold first load on a slow phone link is a few seconds, so 15 s only ends something stuck; the 10 s line has said it's still working by then. `loading.css` has its own at 20 s (`animation: ma-ld-gone 200ms linear 20s both` on `#ma-ld`, with its own keyframes: under `ma-ld-out`'s name, adding `.ma-ld-out` would only retime it), for a page whose `loader.js` never runs; it's later than the script's 15 s and its 700 ms, so the two never meet. *(Changed from the Designer's draft, which had no timer; the brief asks for one.)*
   - Either side can ship first: a new office under the old loader finds no `#ma-ld` and only drops `aria-busy`; the new loader with an old office takes the bundle-from-before path.
7. **Bytes** (gzip -9; CloudFront compresses `/_loader/`): the loader page's `index.html` grows by about 4.0 KB (531 B to 4.6 KB; three marks and the markup), `loading.css` is 2.2 KB as shipped with its comments (1.0 KB without), and `loader.js` grows by 0.9 KB. None of it is the office's: its first load gains only the ready signal and the ending (`docs/sub-status/loading-overlay.md` has the measured figure).

## Consequences
- From the first paint a member sees the theme's own ground, then the mark with a calm glint, never a half-built office; it goes once the office is settled and drawn, or a card or another page is up. A warm load under 500 ms never shows the mark: the bare ground fades in 200 ms.
- A new top-level state (a new page, a card outside `DeskNotice`) must call `loadingDone`, or the overlay stays up to 15 s. A new answer the first frame depends on joins `officeSettled`.
- Platform owns the overlay's markup and CSS; the office owns "ready". ma-desk's harness serves a byte-for-byte copy of the loader page and `loading.css` (`web/harness/loader/`), and its `loadingDone` mirrors `loader.js`'s: change them together. The harness's own `theme.js` sets `data-theme` only for a stored theme, unlike Platform's, so specs that set none see the overlay from the office's `theme/boot` instead of the first paint.
- The kill switch needs Platform's member function and a console dial before it can be flipped.

## Amendment, 2026-11-14: Maia Mode's startup chooser (`sub/mm-maia-room`; Maia Mode CONCEPT.md §2 "At startup", the owner, Nov 12)
For a member with Maia Mode on, its disclosure accepted, allowed (the flag, an adult, Officer or Founder) and "Ask every time I open my office" ticked, the overlay asks "Open your office in: Maia Mode or Desk Mode?", with the last-used mode ready on Enter. Anyone else gets this ADR's overlay, unchanged. Three points change, for that member only:
- **Point 2:** while the question shows, the overlay takes input (the pointer and the keyboard), and the office under it is `inert`; it gets the focus back once the overlay ends.
- **Point 3, a new ending:** an answer pending isn't a stuck load. Nothing ends the question but the member: no timer, no auto-pick, no answer invented. "Ready" is the picked mode's first frame: the canvas drawn for Desk Mode, the room's first frame for Maia Mode (`loadingDone('maia')`, a new reason), which never mounts the canvas.
- **Point 6, the safety times:** the loader's 15 s and `loading.css`'s 20 s don't end the question. The office draws it as an element of its own, `#mm-ld` (`web/src/maiamode/chooser.ts`), after `#ma-ld` and at its z-index, on the same ground with the same mark and track at the same place, so the loader's endings take `#ma-ld` away underneath it and the member sees nothing change. The 10 s line shows under the question too.
- **When it shows:** as the office's entry runs, from this browser's copy of the member's prefs (`ma.maiaMode.v1`, the layout mirror's, wiped on every signed-out state); on a new device once Platform's layout document brings them, while the overlay is up. The office's ready waits for that decision a few seconds at most (`holdLoading` in `chrome/loadingOverlay.ts`, only for a member the flag reaches). The Platform loader page drawing it at its own first paint (before the bundle) is a later step for Platform; the chooser's look and words would move there as they are.
- **Reduced motion:** the question appears by opacity alone (200 ms), the highlight's glow is static and the card jumps; the mode change after picking has no transition; the ending is the 200 ms fade.
- Tests: `web/tests/e2e/maiaMode.shell.spec.ts` ("the startup chooser…", "…another device").

## Amendment, 2026-11-15: the first load's question, and "Ask every time" on by default (`sub/mm-chooser-default`; the owner, Nov 14)
The owner loaded his office once Maia Mode was allowed for him and saw no question, because he hadn't turned it on yet. Two changes, both for members the gate allows (`/v1/me` lists them, under client-config's switch; never a teen, never Free):
- **"Ask every time I open my office" is on unless the member chose otherwise** (`prefs.maiaMode.startup` `{ask: true, mode: "last"}` by default, as ma-platform `desks.md` has it). Accepting the disclosure is the only step: the next load asks. A stored choice (the chooser's checkbox, the menu's "Ask at startup") wins.
- **The first load after Maia Mode is allowed asks a member who never turned it on, once:** the same `#mm-ld`, the same look and words ("Open your office in", Maia Mode or Desk Mode), with no "Ask every time" checkbox, and Desk Mode, where they've been, the default ("Last used · Enter"). Desk Mode, Esc, or "Not now" on the disclosure that Maia Mode opens are answers; either answer is kept in the member's prefs (`prefs.maiaMode.offered: true`, on every device) and it's never asked again. Maia Mode opens §9's disclosure over the desks as the overlay fades (the focus on its title); nothing turns on until it's accepted. A member with a disclosure on record (on, or turned off) is never asked this. It's decided only on `/v1/me`'s own answer, while the overlay is up, with the same points 2, 3 and 6 as the chooser above.
- Tests: `web/tests/e2e/maiaMode.shell.spec.ts` ("the first load after it's allowed asks once…", "Maia Mode from the first question…", "“Not now” on that disclosure…"); `web/src/maiamode/prefs.test.ts` (`offers`, the default).

## Amendment, 2026-11-15: Maia's welcome owns a new member's first load (`sub/mm-welcome`; welcome CONCEPT.md §1, the owner approved the build Nov 15)
- **A new reason, `welcome`.** `ReadyReason` gains `welcome`: the overlay's fade dissolves into the welcome's threshold, which says its own ready once it has painted (`welcome/door.tsx` `welcomePainted`).
- **Holds are per holder.** `holdLoading(on, run, who)` keeps a set of holders (`maia` for Maia Mode's startup, `welcome` for the welcome's decision); the overlay ends once none holds. The welcome holds it only while it may be due for this member (kill switch on, and `/v1/me` not yet saying otherwise), 3 s at most, so an existing member never waits on it.
- **The tour and Maia Mode's first-load question wait** while the welcome decides or runs (`welcomeHolds`); after a welcome the tour doesn't start by itself (its off setting is written; "Show me around" starts it) and the startup chooser is marked answered.
