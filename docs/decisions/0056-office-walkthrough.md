# 0056. The office tour: a short look around on a member's first visit, which they can turn off and back on

**Status:** Proposed (the owner approved the feature, 2026-11-12. The steps, look and motion are the Product Designer's, `~/ma-coord/design-drafts/walkthrough/README.md`; the words are Sales', `WORDS.md` there, used exactly; the build is the UI/UX Engineer's, finished by the Lead Subcontractor on `sub/lsub-walkthrough`)
**Date:** 2026-11-12. **Amended 2026-11-13** (the owner): the whole office in up to twelve steps, a new intro with the Alliance's mark, the hole's corners measured from each target, and the tour only in the logo's menu, with no switch in the account menu. See "Amendment, 2026-11-13" below; decisions 1, 3, 6, 8 and 13 are updated in place.

## Context
The owner, on 2026-11-12: "Before launch we need a tooltip guiding system or walkthrough or whatever its called to introduce someone to their office. They should be able to disable and reenable the walk through as they so choose." It's a launch row (S-157), and it ships on.

The office already has two ways to learn it: the setup circle (S-121), a checklist of what's left to do, and Maia, for questions. The tour does neither job. It's the first minute in a new office: where the desk is, what an app is, where Maia and help are. The owner's standing rule is fewer words, fewer decisions and less noise, so the tour is short and steps aside whenever the member is doing something.

Constraints:
- **The initial JS has little room** (250 KB gzipped, `web/CLAUDE.md` "What loads on demand"), and most visits never need the tour.
- **A finished tour should stay finished on every device** ("a tour finished on a laptop should stay finished on the phone", the Designer). `GET /v1/me` carries `preferences`, but `PUT /v1/me/preferences` takes only `platform-web`'s token (ma-platform contract `auth.md` §3.3), so the office can't write there today. The layout document's `prefs` (design 9.4; contract `desks.md` "Desk UI layout persistence": Desk-owned, stored as it comes, preferences allowed) is written by the office already: the setup circle's flags live there.
- Every shell spec is a fresh member on the harness, so a tour that starts by itself would cover all of them.

## Decision
1. **What it is:** a short look round the whole office, each step a card beside one real thing on screen, under a spotlight: twelve at most for any member (thirteen definitions, `desk` and `desks` being one member's or the other's), fewer where a target isn't there. **What it isn't:** a checklist, tips scattered across the office, a video, or anything that comes back with each release. (Amended 2026-11-13: it was five at most, which the owner found "only 4 things".)

   | # | id | Target | Title | Shown when |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | `intro` | none: a card centred over the office, with the Alliance's mark | Welcome to your office | always |
   | 2 | `desk` / `desks` | the own desks' proxies on screen (`button[data-desk][data-kind="personal"\|"work"]`), each at least 60 % on screen, in one hole | Your desk / Your desks | one own desk / Personal and Work (a phone holds only the shown desk, with the same words) |
   | 3 | `guild` | a Guild Desk's proxy on screen (`@guild`, `button[data-desk][data-kind="guild"]`) | Your guild's desk | the member is in a guild with a desk, and it's on screen |
   | 4 | `scope` | the top bar's Personal, Work, Guild and All (`.topbar .segmented.scope:not(.scope-ghost)`) | Switch desks | more than one desk, and the switch has room in the bar (not folded into the menu) |
   | 5 | `app` | the Browser tile on the first own desk on screen, Personal first (`#proxy-<deskId>:browser`), else its first tile that isn't Courier (`:courier`, which comes later) | Apps | the desk shows tiles (not in carousel mode) |
   | 6 | `move` | the bottom bar's zoom and Fit (`.bottombar .zoom-ctl`) | Move around | the control is there |
   | 7 | `maia` | Maia's round button, `.maia-launcher` | Ask Maia | her button is mounted (client-config `maia`, or a free member's trial) |
   | 8 | `setup` | the setup circle's button (`.setup-circle .su-btn`) | Finish setting up | the circle shows: steps left or something new |
   | 9 | `shield` | the privacy shield (`.topbar button[aria-label="Privacy shield"]`) | Privacy shield | it's in the top bar |
   | 10 | `bell` | the notifications bell (`.topbar [data-testid="bell"]`) | Notifications | it's in the top bar |
   | 11 | `account` | the account menu's button (`.topbar .account-btn`) | Your account | always, in practice |
   | 12 | `help` | the logo's button, `.home-mark-btn[aria-controls="mark-menu"]` | The rest of the Alliance | the logo's menu is on (`office.mark`): the tour's only way back in |

   The words are in `src/tour/steps.ts` (the owner's direction, Nov 13: each step says what the thing is for, a desk and an app included, in place of Sales' first five). A step whose target isn't there (missing, under 60 % on screen, or covered by a window, a sheet or Maia's panel) is left out without a word, and "{n} of {total}" counts only the steps shown. The tour never moves the camera, opens anything or closes anything.
2. **Steps are data** (`src/tour/steps.ts`): `{id, anchor, title, body, placement, pad, radius, when?, phone?}`. `anchor` is a stable CSS selector of the office's own markup (its `fleet-map.json` key noted beside it), `null` for a centred card, or one of three named finders, `@desks` (the own desks' union), `@guild` (a Guild Desk on screen) and `@app` (the tile above). `radius` only overrides the measured corner (decision 3): `circle` for a round button. `placement` is the first side the card tries. `when` is `oneDesk` or `personalAndWork`. `phone: false` leaves a step out on a phone, or `{anchor, placement}` replaces those there.
3. **The look** (the Designer's spec section 2): one fixed box whose `box-shadow` is the scrim, so its inside is the hole, with a 2 px ring of `accent`. The scrim is a new token, `surface.spotlight` (Light `rgba(20,24,32,.28)`, Zen `rgba(12,16,26,.42)`, Dark `rgba(5,6,8,.44)`), lighter than `surface.scrim`, since the tour is about the office. The intro's hole is a 0 x 0 point at the middle with no ring. **The hole's corners run parallel to its target's** (amended 2026-11-13; the owner: the highlight's corners didn't match what it pointed at): its radius is the target's own corner on screen plus the step's pad, never more than half the hole's short side, so a pill stays a pill (`engine.ts` `holeRadius`, `cssRadius`). `TourLayer.tsx` measures it: a desk at `G.panelRadius` (22) and a tile at `G.tileRadius` (18, its icon face) or `G.widgetRadius` (20, larger sizes), from `canvas/layout.ts`, times the camera's scale (the proxy's width on screen over its own; tile proxies carry their size class as `data-level`); anything of the office's chrome by its computed `border-top-left-radius`. The card is the menus' popover surface: 320 px (the intro 400, with the Alliance's mark, the theme's own file as the top bar's, over a 20 px title, and its × in the corner), a title, one line, a × named "Close the tour", "{n} of {total}", a quiet button (Skip on step 1, Back after it) and the primary (Next, or Done on the last step), and under a hairline the quiet link "Don't show this again", hidden on the last step. A caret points at the target. The card takes the first side with room (below, above, right, left), 14 px off the hole and 16 px from the window's edges; with no room it sits inside the hole at its lower right.
4. **Motion** (the mark menu's family): start 260 ms, a step 340 ms (the card fades, the hole glides 280 ms, the new card fades in toward its target), the end 200 ms. CSS transitions on the hole, the Web Animations API on the card; a handful of writes per step, never per frame. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: nothing moves or fades; each change shows on the next frame.
5. **A modal dialog.** The card is `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, named by its title and described by its line and count. The rest of the office is inert (`holdOffice`, as Account settings does). Focus goes to Next on start and stays on the primary button from step to step; Back keeps focus, except arriving at step 1, where it goes to Next. Tab and Shift+Tab cycle the card's four controls. Enter on the card itself is Next. **Esc** ends it as Skip does and stops there, so the office's own Esc (show all desks) never hears it; no other key reaches the office either. A press on the scrim or in the hole does nothing; `wheel` and `touchmove` are stopped, so nothing behind scrolls or zooms (only the card itself scrolls, on a screen too short for it). Each step change is said once, "{n} of {total}. {title}. {line}", through a polite `role="status"` region inside the card (VoiceOver hears nothing outside an open modal dialog, and the shell's region is in the office the tour holds inert); the card itself isn't live, so nothing is said twice. Leaving, the card stops being modal, so the shell's note after "Don't show this again" is heard. At the end the office is let go first, then focus goes back where it was (the mark's button after "Take the tour"), or to the canvas if that's gone. **A menu open anyway comes first** (amended 2026-11-14, the Product Designer's BRAND-19/20 read: the account menu sat dimmed under the intro): a menu or popover that shows while the tour is up (`.menu`, `role="menu"`, the logo's, the bottom bar's zoom; as one does when it opens in a race with the tour's start) steps the tour back (`.tour.yield`: no scrim, no card, no presses, not modal), the office is let go and no key is kept, so the menu is the member's, with the keyboard back where it was before the card took it; as the menu shuts the tour is back on the same step, the office held again and the keyboard on Next. A sheet that menu opens still ends it as Skip does, leaving the keyboard where the member put it.
6. **What each control does:**

   | Control | Ends it | Writes |
   |---|---|---|
   | Next / Back | no | nothing |
   | Done | yes | off |
   | Skip, ×, Esc | yes | nothing: it starts again the next time the office opens |
   | Don't show this again | yes | off, and the shell's passing notice says once "Turned off. You can take the tour again from the logo's menu, top left." |
   | "Take the tour" (the logo's menu) | starts now, whatever the setting | nothing |

   There is no switch to turn it back on (amended 2026-11-13): "Don't show this again" still stops it starting by itself, and the logo's menu always starts it.
7. **When it starts by itself:** once per page load, only when all hold: signed in; `/v1/me`, the desk list and client-config are here and the kill switch isn't off; the canvas has painted (`ma:first-canvas-paint`); the layout document has been read (`layoutDocRead()`); the setting isn't off; an own desk is listed and the account isn't closed; and **the office is quiet** for 800 ms: the tab visible; no window, carousel stage, open tile, sheet, modal, menu or Maia panel open; the notices stack holds nothing but the new-version notice; no desk notice; no own desk setting up, held or queued; no drag, keyboard edit or camera move; and the loader's loading overlay (`#ma-ld`, ADR 0058) gone or going. If quiet hasn't come 30 s after the office was ready, it doesn't start in this page load. While it runs, a new notice (sign-in, billing, safety, "Can't reach…") or a modal opening ends it as Skip does; a target going away moves it to the next step that has one, or ends it with nothing written. Existing members, the owner included, have no setting yet, so each sees it once on their next visit.
8. **Where it lives: the logo's menu only** (amended 2026-11-13; the owner wanted one way in, not two):
   - **"Take the tour"** is a button row in the logo's (mark's) menu, in its first group after "All desks" (alone there without it). Choosing it shuts the menu at once and starts the tour, whatever the setting, with focus going back to the logo at its end. The menu prefetches the tour's chunk when it opens. It shows wherever the tour is on: the kill switch isn't off, the member has a Personal or Work desk, and the account isn't closed.
   - **The account menu has nothing of the tour:** no "Show the office tour" switch and no "Take the tour" row. With the logo's menu's kill switch off there's no way in and no `help` step, until it's back.
   - "Don't show this again" (or Done) is how a member stops it starting by itself; the setting it writes is kept in the three layers below, unchanged.
9. **Remembered per member, three layers, read in this order:**
   1. **Platform's account preference, once it exists:** `GET /v1/me` `preferences.officeTour` `{"off": boolean, ...}`. Its presence (even `null`) is the sign that Platform lets the office write it, and then the office also sends `PUT /v1/me/preferences {"officeTour": {"off": <bool>}}` with every change. Absent (today): never sent, so no `403` is ever asked for. The proposed contract is in `docs/sub-status/walkthrough.md`; nothing changes in ma-platform here.
   2. **The layout document's `prefs.tourOff`** (`true` or `false`), synced to every browser today, with no new route and no `SCHEMA_VERSION` change. Written only once the document has been read, as the setup circle's steps are (`setup/prefs.ts`), so it can't write over what another device saved.
   3. **This browser's record,** `localStorage['ma.tour.v1'] = {who: <userId>, off: boolean}`, written first, before either of the others, and read for this member only. It's one of the layout mirror's keys (`LAYOUT_MIRROR_KEYS`), so every signed-out state and change of account wipes it (ISS-67, ISS-89), and it's written only while `layoutMirrorWritable()`. When the document has been read and has no value yet, this browser's goes up to it. This layer alone already keeps the tour from starting twice on one device.

   **A change of member in a tab** (another tab's sign-in, ISS-67 and ISS-89's `onMemberChange`): the last member's tour closes at once with nothing written, and the page forgets its decision and what it sent Platform; it decides afresh for the new member once their `/v1/me`, desk list and own document (`layoutDocMember`) are in.

   Missing everywhere is a first visit. Nothing else is kept: no step reached, no times, no counts. Skip keeps nothing: the tour starts by itself once per page load anyway.
10. **Phones (under 600 px):** the card spans the width less 12 px a side, docked to the bottom (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`), or to the top when the hole's bottom comes within 24 px of it (Maia's step); no caret; `max-height: calc(100dvh - 24px)`, scrolling inside. Back, Skip, Next and Done are 44 px tall, × a 44 px square, the off link's row 44 px. Same steps: the desk step's hole is the desk on screen; the app step is a tile in the phone's grid.
11. **Themes, tiers and bands:** tokens only, so Light, Zen and Dark follow. Every tier gets the same tour; Maia's step shows only where her button does (a free member's trial, or client-config's `maia` block). Teens get the same tour and words. A member's bandwidth state changes nothing: the circle isn't a step, and a pause notice in the stack holds a first start until it's gone, as any notice does.
12. **Lazy:** the first load holds only `src/tour/door.ts` (the kill switch, the setting's read, the once-a-page decision, `startTour` and `prefetchTour`). The rest (the quiet check, the steps and words, placement, keys and focus, the inert hold, the motion, and the writes in `tour/prefs.ts`) is the tour's own chunk, with its own stylesheet (`tour.css?url`, as the simplified view's), so the app's one stylesheet gains only the spotlight token. The chunk is fetched only when the tour may start by itself, on "Take the tour", or as the mark menu opens. A member who has finished the tour never fetches it again.
13. **Kill switch:** client-config `walkthrough: {enabled: false}`; absent is on. Off: no start by itself and no "Take the tour"; the stored setting is kept; a tour already running finishes normally. The harness sends it off by default; the tour's specs turn it on.
14. **The fleet's keys** (`public/fleet-map.json`, checked by `fleet-map.shell.spec.ts`): `office.tour` (the card), `office.tour.next` (Next, or Done), `office.tour.skip` (Skip on step 1, Back after it: the quiet button), `office.tour.close` (×), `office.tour.off` ("Don't show this again", which ends it for good on a fresh test account) and `office.mark.tour` ("Take the tour").

## Amendment, 2026-11-13
The owner, on the first tour: its first card was "lazy and uninspired"; it showed "only 4 things"; its highlight's corners didn't match what it pointed at; the app step never said what an app is; "Open it from any browser" didn't say what a desk is for; and the tour had two ways in (the account menu's switch and "Take the tour", and the logo's menu) where he wanted only the logo's menu. So (`sub/tour-office`, desk release 20):
- **Steps:** the table in decision 1, each shown only where its target is on screen for this member: a desk and an app are explained, then moving around, Maia, setup, the privacy shield, the bell, the account menu and the logo's menu, where the tour lives.
- **Intro:** "Welcome to your office", 400 px, with the Alliance's mark (the theme's own file, as the top bar uses).
- **Corners:** measured from each target, decision 3.
- **One way in:** decision 8. The account menu's "Show the office tour" switch and its "Take the tour" row are gone; "Don't show this again" names the logo's menu.
- **The release gate** (`e2e/` and `web/tests/real-desk/rt-csp-windows.mjs`): the shared test account meets the tour like any member, so `focusTestDesk` (e2e `tests/lib.ts`) and `closeTour` (`rt-lib.mjs`) close it with its own close button before the first key, which writes nothing.

## Consequences
- A new member gets one short look around, once, and is never asked again unless they ask.
- The setting follows the member today on the existing document; if Platform opens `preferences.officeTour`, the office uses it with no Desk release.
- The first load grows by a few hundred bytes. The tour's chunk and stylesheet load only when it runs.
- Pre-launch accounts, the owner's included, see the tour once after it ships.
- The fleet's journeys on fresh accounts meet the tour; `office.tour.off` ends it for good. So does the release gate's shared test account, which closes it with `office.tour.close` (nothing written).
- A member who chose "Don't show this again" can't make it start by itself again; they take it from the logo's menu whenever they like (amended 2026-11-13).
- Only the System Engineer adds this ADR's row to the index.
