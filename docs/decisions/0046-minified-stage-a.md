# 0046: The simplified view's desk side, Stage A

- **Status:** Proposed. Built on `sub/minified-stage-a`, in no release; on by default with a kill switch once merged.
- **Date:** 2026-11-07
- **Owner:** Desk Engineer
- **Builds on:** [0021](0021-minified-websites-structure-based.md), [0030](0030-browser-modes-local-minified-60fps-normal.md), [0041](0041-deskd-drives-cdp-directly.md), design §7, task 47, and the Security Analyst's task 47 read (T47-1 to T47-11).

## Context
The owner wants the simplified browsing view built properly: elegant, useful, with video, and with no ads or spam. Its desk side has to turn a real page into a document the Desk app renders, do the member's clicks and typing on the real page without ever acting on the wrong thing, remember per site what the member chose, and give video islands their boxes. The prototype proved the idea and the Security Analyst's read showed what can't carry over: a main-world extractor a page can disarm, a click guard that sees only the top frame, a fall-back click, and sensitive fields rendered as real inputs in the office.

## Decision
1. **A package of its own, `browser/minified`,** reaching the tabs only through a narrow bridge (`TabAccess`) that the Browser's `BrowserTabs` gives it: a tab the Browser lists, deskd's session on it, frame sessions Chromium attached under it, and events. No RPC takes a CDP method or a script.
2. **Two scripts, both constants in the jar, in deskd's own isolated world (`ma-minify`), in every frame:** the distiller (`distiller.js`, the prototype's `extract.js` byte for byte at the commit `distiller.pin` names: one distiller, not two. `services/scripts/sync-distiller.sh` copies it, `DistillerPinTest` fails on any drift, and `Scripts` adapts it as it loads: the click path and page writes cut, deskd's export, Readability, and a small built-in set of cosmetic rules where the prototype's server sent EasyList; deskd calls only `extractText`, `lookup` and `setRules`) and deskd's own half (`proxy.js`: the guard, fingerprints, sensitive fields, islands, the long-poll for changes). deskd calls one constant function with JSON arguments. The prototype's own click path is removed from the shipped distiller.
3. **MDoc v1** is built in Java, field by field, from the distiller's untrusted output: a closed schema (`mdoc-schema.json`, checked by a test), cleaned and bounded text, http(s) URLs without userinfo, images by opaque id, 512 KiB.
4. **The action proxy refuses rather than guesses.** An action names the node, its fingerprint, its block and the MDoc's rev. Every other frame, cross-site ones included through auto-attach held at their start, is armed to cancel trusted input for longer than any action takes; a frame that can't be armed refuses. The element is fingerprinted again after the scroll, in the hit-test call, and through the press itself, where a change cancels the click; frames near the point refuse, by Chromium's own boxes (`DOM.getFrameOwner`, then `DOM.getBoxModel`), so one in a closed shadow root or another process counts (T47D-2); nothing that changed between the arm and the press is pressed; press and release go with no move; a click counts only if the element itself got it. No re-locate, ever. A frame in another process isn't in its parent's frame tree, so Chromium's frame targets are read beside the trees, and one deskd holds no session for refuses (review 6). The reply doesn't wait for the frames' disarm, which the tab's next action does wait for; another frame's world is made once per document, and trusted only while it answers with its own random id (T47N-2); Page is enabled on each frame session before deskd's script, so every frame document starts armed, and a re-read of the frames past 1 s refuses (T47N-1); and an action goes ahead of a look at the page that hasn't started (B8's three cuts, none of which shortens what any frame is armed for).
5. **Sensitive fields are never fields:** password, one-time code, card (selects included) and masked fields, and payment providers' frames, become a "Sign in on (origin)" run that switches to the stream, judged on every snapshot, again at focus and again at the key. Signals only add, and stick to the site for the session.
6. **Images come only from what the tab loaded** (`Page.getResourceContent`), sniffed for a raster type, never fetched and never decoded by deskd. The Browser's CDP client gained a long-reply read for that one call.
7. **Per-site memory lives on the desk** (`/data/desk/minified-sites.json`): atomic, capped at 2000 hosts and 512 KiB, 90 days (at load too), automatic fallbacks 7 days, cleared by `sites.clear`. So does the ad-blocking off list (`/data/desk/adblock-off`, 0640, for the image's blocklist, emptied by `sites.clear`; Security `T47S-3`).
8. **On, with a runtime kill switch** (the owner's rule, Nov 7: finished features ship on; switches get us out of trouble): `/data/desk/minified-switch`, not a Spring condition, which AOT would freeze. With no file everything is on; `off` turns everything off and is fully inert, and `media-actions-off` and `consent-reject-off` turn off one action each. Security blockers are fixed, not switched off: the media verbs and the consent press ship on with every one of the Security Analyst's conditions implemented and tested, and `T47D-1` fixed.
9. **The client contract** is `API.md` §14: RPCs and a per-tab topic, not the `mdoc` channel, which stays reserved.
10. **Never more than the real page shows the member** (the Product Designer's round two, the owner's default until they decide). deskd's own half keys the text the member can't see (not drawn, clipped away, or under a wall), and the builder leaves hidden text out and ends the document at a wall, whatever the distiller sends: a paywall with one `paywall` block, a terms wall with the fallback `terms_wall`. An island is live only while nothing but its player is over it.
11. **A site is its registrable domain** for the member's choices as for sensitivity, whatever host a client names.

## Consequences
- The renderer (UI/UX) builds against §14 and the schema file; the island's encoder crop (System) against `browser.minified.island`'s box and epoch.
- Every action costs a frame-tree read and an arm per frame, twice; on ad-heavy pages that's the bulk of deskd's overhead toward B8, unmeasured yet.
- New frames of a simplified tab wait on deskd at their start; when nobody has used the view on a tab for a minute, the hold and the script come off.
- Not done here: HTTP auth before a commit, WebAuthn and Payment Request sheets, which no CDP event shows without intercepting the member's traffic or replacing their authenticator; a signed, updatable sensitive-site list; the blocklist; recipes; Chromium's own clear-history reaching `sites.clear`.
- Lever 2's freeze (ADR 0045): every call, and a SUB's first look, is work that thaws Chromium first and holds off a freeze; the topic's watch for changes is not, and never thaws it. The freeze closes deskd's connection, and the view goes with it.
