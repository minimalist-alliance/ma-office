# 0055. Each app zooms on its own: a slider by its expand button, Cmd/Ctrl+scroll and a pinch over it

**Status:** Proposed (the owner's ask, approved, 2026-11-12; the Lead's spec; the calls below are the UI/UX Engineer's, the deskd interface the Desk Engineer's). The office's half merged to `main` from `sub/lsub-app-zoom` (the Lead Subcontractor, 2026-11-12, after a separate review; `docs/sub-status/app-zoom.md`), in no release yet. deskd's half (S-156-BR) and the desk app view's gestures (ZOOM-SBX) merged from `sub/browser-zoom` (2026-11-14, `docs/sub-status/browser-zoom.md`), in no release yet: deskd API.md §10, "Zoom".
**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Amends:** the pinch rule in `web/CLAUDE.md` (`canvas/pinch.ts`: a pinch over a window magnified nothing and moved nothing)

## Context
The owner, 2026-11-12: "For every app, I need to be able to zoom in and out with a popout slider that shows up next to the expand button on the top of each app or command scroll (ctl scroll for windows)." The Lead added that a trackpad pinch over an app zooms it too, and that it ships on after the Designer's read of the button and slider.

What there was: the desk camera's zoom (the bottom bar, the wheel and a pinch on the canvas), which makes everything on the desk bigger or smaller, and the member's own browser's page zoom (Ctrl and the wheel off the canvas, Ctrl+plus), which makes the whole office bigger, its bars and every desk with it. Neither makes one app's text bigger. A pinch over a window did nothing (`wheelPinchGoes` said `stop`), so the browser wouldn't magnify the page under it.

Apps come in two kinds here:
- **Office apps** are drawn by the Desk app itself: Notebook, Files, the Agent, Courier's empty state, Commons, and desk apps (ADR 0054, such as the Bible), which draw in a sandboxed `srcdoc` frame inside their window.
- **Streamed apps** are pictures of the desk: the desk Browser, whose `<video>` shows the desk's Chromium. Making that picture bigger would blur it, and the page under it wouldn't re-lay out.

## Decision
1. **The control.** Every app's title bar has a zoom button beside its expand button: a window's head, before "Fill the screen" / "Restore window" (`windows/AppWindow.tsx`); an open tile's head, before "Open in its window" (`opentile/`); the carousel's head on a larger screen and each app's own head in the phone's carousel (`carousel/`). Its glyph is a circle holding two corner brackets, top right and bottom left, pointing out, drawn as the bar's other icons are (24-unit view, `currentColor`, the same stroke, caps and joins), so it reads apart from the expand button's square. It opens a small popout like the office's other popovers (one at a time, a press outside or Esc closes it): a slider from 50 to 200 % in 10 % steps, and the level as a button that puts it back to 100 %. Keyboard: the button is a Tab stop named "Zoom, 120%"; the slider is an `input type=range` with `aria-valuetext` "120%", the arrows step 10, Home and End go to 50 and 200, Esc closes and gives the keyboard back to the button. The same in every bandwidth band. **The slider is drawn from tokens** (2026-11-14, the Product Designer's BRAND-19/20 read: the browser's own track is a near-black stripe on Zen's and Dark's popout): the office's one range style, `.ma-range` in `app.css`, a 4 px `surface.fillStrong` track, its filled part and the 16 px thumb the accent with a 2 px `surface.overlay` rim, the office's focus ring; the theme sliders use it too.
2. **Cmd/Ctrl+scroll and a pinch.** Over an app (its frame, head included), Cmd and the wheel (macOS) and Ctrl and the wheel (Windows, Linux) zoom that app in 10 % steps, the wheel's deltas added up so a trackpad's small ones don't jump and a mouse's notch is one step. A trackpad pinch over an app (a wheel with `ctrlKey` and no real Control key, or Safari's GestureEvents) zooms it too, instead of `stop`. The listener isn't passive and calls `preventDefault`, so neither the desk camera nor the page moves. `canvas/pinch.ts` routes it: `wheelPinchGoes` answers `app` over an element with `data-zoom-app`, which each app's frame carries while its app can zoom, and hands the event to the zoom chunk's handler. **A desk app's own view** (ADR 0054, the Bible) is a sandboxed `srcdoc` frame whose wheel and pinch are its own document's, so the office's listener never hears them. The office's own `boot.js` in the frame sends them up (ZOOM-SBX): on a `MessagePort` of their own, handed to the office beside the budget's probe in `ma.ports`, which boot sends before the view runs, so the view never has the port. `ma.ports` and `ma.boot` carry a per-frame nonce the office puts in the `srcdoc` (`frame.ts` `frameNonce`) and boot reads and removes before the view runs; the office takes `ma.ports` once and only with it, and closes the ports of anything it refuses, so the view can't pass its own ports off as boot's. Only a trusted event (`isTrusted`), only Ctrl or Cmd with the wheel or Safari's pinch (not while a finger touches, as in the office), only one the view didn't take itself (`preventDefault` on its document or below, as an office app keeps its own; boot listens on the frame's window after the view's start), and only numbers: `{w, m, k}` (the wheel's `deltaY`, its `deltaMode`, whether Control or Cmd was down) or `{g}` (a pinch's step). No point, no target, no key but Control, nothing of the page; the natives boot reads with are kept before the view runs, so a view that replaces them can't change what's read or sent. Boot calls `preventDefault` on what it sends, so the office isn't magnified. The office reads it as the frame's (`deskapps/host/viewZoom.ts`): a plain object of exactly those keys, each held to its range (a delta to 2,000 px, a step to 0.1..10), at most 240 a second, and hands it to `pinch.ts`'s `viewZoom` for the window the frame is in, which zooms it as any app. An independent Security read (Nov 14) found the view could first pass off ports of its own (fixed by the nonce and the early `ma.ports`) and confirmed the fix: only zoom gestures cross (`docs/sub-status/browser-zoom.md`).
3. **Off the apps, everything as today.** A pinch on the canvas or the bars zooms the camera; a real Ctrl+wheel off the canvas is the browser's page zoom; menus, dialogs and fields that aren't in an app keep `stop`; Cmd+wheel off an app is the browser's. A touch pinch stays the canvas's or the Browser picture's own.
4. **Office apps re-lay out, never a scaled picture.** The level is CSS `zoom` on the app's body (`.window-body` in a window, an open tile and the carousel), standard in Chrome, Safari and Firefox 126+: text stays text, crisp at every level, and hit targets grow with it. A desk app's sandboxed `srcdoc` frame is inside that body, and its content re-lays out with it, crisp, in Chromium (its `innerWidth` halves at 200 %, `devicePixelRatio` 2) and Firefox (the same width, `devicePixelRatio` 1); WebKit wasn't tried (it doesn't run on the build machine). So no desk app contract message is needed for those two. **Commons doesn't zoom yet:** its board sizes its canvas from its box on screen, so under CSS zoom it draws at twice its size at 200 % and the pointer lands in the wrong place; it has no button (`zoom/level.ts` `NO_ZOOM`) until its board lays out in its own pixels (the Guild Engineer's `apps/commons/web/src/board/engine.ts`).
5. **Streamed apps set the desk's own zoom.** For the desk Browser the level is Chromium's own page zoom, set by deskd, so the page re-lays out sharp on the desk and the picture stays one to one. deskd's interface (the Desk Engineer's, deskd API.md sections 4 and 10):
   - `browser.zoom.get` → `{level}`, asked once per session, as its stream comes up (after `browser.stream.set`, which starts the Browser, and the tabs);
   - `browser.zoom.set {level}` → `{level}`, an integer percent from 50 to 200, a mutation (with `idem`, and input, like the Browser's other drivers), coalesced to one call per 100 ms while the slider or the wheel moves; the office steps 10 % at a time there too (the Desk Engineer offered Chromium's own list, 50 67 75 80 90 100 110 125 150 175 200: one rule for every app reads simpler), and a level the desk reports off that grid (a key chord the page took) shows as it is, the next step going to the grid;
   - topic `browser.view` gains `zoom: <level>`, sent whenever it changes, whoever changed it (a key chord forwarded to the page, another session), and the office adopts it.
   - that field is the interface's change event: the office subscribes to no `browser.zoom` topic of its own (the UI/UX Engineer's first proposal named one; the Desk Engineer's answer, 14:54 MT, put it on `browser.view`, the topic the Browser already holds). A `zoom` on `browser.view` before `browser.zoom.get` has answered is ignored.
   - **A desk without it** (every deskd today): `browser.zoom.get` is answered `not_found` ("No such method", API.md §4), or `forbidden`; either means no zoom for that session. `unavailable`, a session that drops, and no answer ask again on the next session. A refused `browser.zoom.set` asks `get` again, and the desk's answer stands.

   One level for the whole Browser on that desk: every member tab and site, now and later; the Agent's hidden tabs stay at 100 %. Over the Browser's picture, Cmd/Ctrl+wheel and a pinch become `browser.zoom.set` instead of going to the desk as input (`browser/streamInput.ts`). Until the desk answers `browser.zoom.get`, the Browser has no zoom button (no control that does nothing) and the wheel over its picture goes to the desk as before.
6. **Memory.** Office apps: per app, per member, on this browser, in one localStorage key, `ma.appZoom.v1`, holding `{app: level}` (100 % isn't kept), read once when the zoom chunk loads, written only while the layout's mirror may be (`layoutMirrorWritable`), and in `MEMBER_CACHE_KEYS`, so every signed-out state and a change of account take it (ISS-89: nothing of one member's shows for the next). Per app, not per window or per desk: the Notebook is the same size on every desk. A desk app is keyed by its app id (`app:<id>`); every agent is the Agent app, keyed `agent`. The Browser's level is the desk's own (deskd keeps it across restarts and moves), so the office keeps none of it: whatever browser the member comes from, the desk's level shows.
7. **What doesn't change.** The desk camera's own zoom and its controls in the bottom bar; ADR 0064's constant-size chrome (titles, heads, the zoom button itself, the bars); the bars.
8. **Bundle.** The first load grows by the routing only (`canvas/pinch.ts`, the cache key); the button, the popout, the level math and the handler are lazy chunks with the frames that show them.
9. **Reduced motion.** Nothing about an app's zoom moves, with or without `prefers-reduced-motion`: the popout is there at once (as the office's other menus), and a new level lays the app out at once, with no transition on `zoom`.

## Alternatives rejected
- **CSS `transform: scale()` on the app.** Every app would be a scaled picture of itself: blurry text between levels, hit targets that don't match what they look like, and an app that doesn't re-lay out to its frame, so zooming in would push it off the frame's edge.
- **The browser's own page zoom.** It zooms the whole office, bars, desks and every other app with it, which the member can already do; it's not per app.
- **Scaling the Browser's picture.** Blurry, and the page wouldn't re-lay out; the desk's own zoom keeps it sharp.
- **Per window or per desk.** The Lead's call is per app: one Notebook size for the member. Per desk can come later if it reads better in use.

## Consequences
- Each app can be read at the member's own size, without changing the rest of the office.
- A pinch over a window now does something: it zooms that app, where before it did nothing.
- The Browser's zoom follows the desk, not the browser the member uses; an office app's follows this browser, not the desk.
- An app laid out in CSS pixels sees a smaller or larger width at other levels (a window 600 px wide at 200 % lays out at 300 px), so apps answer to their own width (their container queries) as in a narrow window.
- Tests: `zoom/level.test.ts`, `zoom/store.test.ts`, `canvas/pinch.test.ts`, `state/memberCaches.test.ts`, `deskapps/host/viewZoom.test.ts`; `appzoom.shell.spec.ts` (with Ctrl, Cmd and a pinch over the Bible's view, and a view that can't forge one), the zoom keys in `fleet-map.shell.spec.ts`; deskd's `BrowserZoomTest` and `BrowserZoomChromiumIT`.
