# Draft. Courier's transports through the relay: the desk's address and members' sealed messages

**Status:** Proposed (draft; numbered from the Desk range 0110 to 0119 when it's accepted). **Built** on `sub/mm-courier-transports` (the launch plan's Wave 2.5): deskd's `courier/relay/`, `courier.db` schema version 4, the test kit's `courier-send` and door notes. In no release, on no desk; Courier stays behind `COURIER_READY`.
**Date:** 2026-11-14
**Decision:** the owner, Nov 12: Courier is prelaunch at the production bar ("VERY solid and capable... pretty much production ready, no MVP"). The coordinator's brief for CR-2.5: drain sealed envelopes when the desk wakes (never wake a desk for a message), submit outbound to `courier-send` behind an adapter seam, delivery notes, sealed member-to-member messages with key pinning, safety numbers, invites and the picker's rules.
**Builds on:** [draft-courier-envelope](draft-courier-envelope.md) (the keys and the envelope library; its CK-I5 and CK-I6 are answered here), [draft-courier-relay](draft-courier-relay.md), [draft-courier-address](draft-courier-address.md), [draft-courier-store](draft-courier-store.md) (the sink, the queue), [draft-courier-channels](draft-courier-channels.md); ADR [0016](0016-chat-channels-and-delivery.md) (one thread per person, the default delivery), [0017](0017-sealed-relay-desk-to-desk.md).
**Contract:** ma-platform `docs/contracts/relay.md` 0.4.0 (merged `ee1fdd57`), §2 (the wire format) and §3 (the agent routes). Section 9 below is what this side proposes for 0.4.1.

## Context
- The relay holds two kinds of sealed envelope for a desk: `mail` (an email to the desk's own address, sealed by Platform's mail door) and `ma` (a message from another member's desk). Both wait while the desk sleeps; neither may wake it.
- The envelope library (`courier-envelope`) seals, signs and opens; the store's sink keeps a message once by `(medium, externalId)`; the queue sends with undo, retries and the "it may have gone" mark. What was missing is the transport between them and the relay: when to collect, how to decide who sent what, what to pin, what to refuse, and how to send.
- `courier-send` (Platform's submission for mail from desk addresses, CR-2.4, on SES) and the mail door's `mail` payload are contract 0.4.1, not written yet.

## Decisions

### 1. One transport for both kinds, in deskd
`courier/relay/RelayTransport`: one drain of one relay, so one ack path and one badge count (draft-courier-relay point 1). It owns two store accounts, `desk` (kind `desk`, the desk's address as email) and `ma` (kind `ma`), made from `GET /v1/agent/courier-addresses` at start and on `notify relay {reason: address}`; the desk's closed addresses stay its own addresses (`own_addresses`), so mail and messages sealed to an old name still open and still count as the member's. What the platform said is kept in the store's cursor table, so a restart without the network still opens envelopes.

### 2. When it collects, and how
- **When:** at deskd's start (a desk waking: origin `catchup`), on the gateway's `notify relay` (`envelopes`; `address` reads the addresses first), and every 10 minutes while running as the contract's fallback for a lost push. A failed drain retries with backoff from 15 seconds to 10 minutes, honouring `Retry-After`. **Nothing in deskd ever wakes a desk**: a sleeping desk's envelopes wait sealed, and the platform's count is the badge. One drain runs at a time.
- **Store, then ack:** a page's good messages go into the sink in one transaction, each with its tombstone (section 3); only then are their envelopes acked. A store that can't write (a full disk) keeps nothing and acks nothing. Pages of 100; a batch is stored early past 16 MiB of bodies, and at once after any body over 4 MiB, so the drain holds about one large message at a time in deskd's small heap; a drop is read into an array of exactly its signed length (Security CT-M3).
- **No envelope stops a drain** (Security CT-H1): each is handled on its own; one that throws anything unexpected waits, and after 3 drains is refused for good.
- **Three outcomes per envelope:**
  - **Store**, then ack.
  - **Ack without storing**, counted by reason (never with content): refused for good (it doesn't open, isn't for this desk, is too old or dated more than a day ahead, doesn't read, a forged sender, a sender off the Courier domain or with no keys to check, an unknown kind), held (section 4), a door note applied (section 6), or a copy already taken in.
  - **Wait** (no ack; the next drain sees it again): the directory unreachable, rate-limited or paused, a drop's link not issued today (the contract's daily caps), the door's key not known yet (or its signature failing for less than an hour: only the door writes mail envelopes, so that's the platform's lag, Security CT-L2), a drop that didn't come down, the held caps reached, the channel switched off.
- **Each envelope is looked at in order:** its relay id's tombstone (a lost ack's copy is acked at once); then HPKE open with the desk's addresses and box keys (the library tries the closed addresses and retired keys); the payload's kind against the envelope's (CK-L1); the age; the sink key's tombstone; the sender (section 4 for `ma`, the door's key for `mail`); the payload's fields; a drop, which must be one of the envelope's own drops at the size the relay gave, opened and digest-checked through the library before any byte is used.
- **The directory once per sender per drain, and only for members' addresses** (Security CT-M1): a `p.from` off the Courier domain is refused before any lookup; a flood naming one address costs one lookup; after a `rate_limited` every other new sender waits without a call; a lasting refusal (anything but `429`, a `5xx` or `courier_paused`) refuses the envelope, and `address_closed` is remembered a day, `address_unknown` an hour.

### 3. Replay (Security CK-I6)
- **Age:** an `ma` payload whose `at` is older than the relay's 30-day wait plus a day of skew is refused (`stale`); `mail`, 90 days plus a day. One dated more than a day ahead is refused too (`future`: its tombstone would never expire, Security CT-I5).
- **Tombstones** (`relay_seen`): every envelope taken in leaves its sink key (`ma1:<from>:<id>` for a member's message; `ExternalIds.email("desk", null, raw)` for mail, so the door sealing the same message twice is one message; `note1:<id>` for a note) in the sink's own transaction, kept until `max(at, now) + wait + a day`, past which the age check refuses a replay anyway. A message the member deleted for good can't come back while the relay could still hand its ciphertext over. The relay's own envelope id is tombstoned too, in the same transaction for a stored message (Security CT-I3), so a copy after a lost ack is acked without being opened or downloaded again.
- **The payload's id is the message's own** on the sending side, the same on every try, so a resend after the relay forgot its idempotency key is still one message at the receiver.

### 4. Pins, safety numbers, invites (spike Q-C2 to Q-C4; draft-courier-envelope §6)
- **Pins are on the identity key**, one row per member address in `ma_pins`: `unverified` (trusted on first use), `verified` (an invite's fingerprint matched, or the member compared the safety number), or `changed` (another identity showed up: held as `new_*`).
- **Receiving:** a message is accepted only under the pinned identity. With no pin, the directory's keys for `p.from` are fetched (binding checked, the answer's `address` must be the one asked), the signature verified, and the identity pinned unverified; the message is a stranger's first, so it lands in Requests. Signed by a new identity the directory now gives for a pinned address, it's **held** (`ma_held`, kept out of the store, at most 200 messages and 64 MiB an address and 2,000 and 256 MiB a desk, past which it waits in the relay rather than being lost, Security CT-L1; deleted after 30 days or when the sender is blocked) and the pin marked changed. Signed by anything else, it's a **forgery** and refused. A message from one of the desk's own addresses verifies only with the desk's own identity.
- **Sending:** sealed to the pin's keys, re-read from the directory every 6 hours (a box key rotates every 180 days, and the same identity's new box key is the same contact). A directory answer under another identity marks the pin changed. **A changed pin blocks sending to that address:** the queue holds the message (`retry`, `key_changed`, "Waiting to send") and the picker offers no default.
- **The member checks:** `courier.contact.verify {address, safetyNumber}` takes the 60 digits the member compared. The pinned identity's number marks it verified (a change shown meanwhile is dropped, with what it held); a changed pin's new number pins the new identity, verified, releases its held messages into the store as they came, and makes the waiting sends due at once. Anything else is `conflict`.
- **Invites** (`ma://courier/<address>#k=<fingerprint>`, or the office's own link read into `courier.contacts.add {name, fingerprint?, displayName?}`, the shape `sub/mm-courier-ui` proposed; without a fingerprint the address is pinned on first use): `courier.invite.open` looks the address up; the keys must have the link's fingerprint, and then they're pinned verified and the contact is made and let in. An unchecked or already changed pin takes the keys the link vouches for (`changed_verified`, its held messages released); **a pin the member checked under another identity isn't replaced**: it's marked changed and the answer is `conflict` `{reason: key_changed}`, so the member confirms with the safety number (Security CT-M2). A fingerprint that doesn't match pins nothing (`invite_mismatch`, with `pinned`), unless it's the pinned identity with a box key this desk itself recorded for it (`rotated`: the pin keeps its last 4 box keys, so an old link is recognised, never assumed).
- **The platform's directory is trusted only at a first contact without an invite** (C9's honest limit). Past it, a key it swaps is a visible change.

### 5. Sending
- **`ma`:** one envelope per recipient, the payload `{v, kind: message, id, from, to, at, text | drop, subject?, members?}` signed and sealed to that recipient's pinned keys; text that won't fit a 64 KiB envelope goes in the ciphertext drop (reserved, uploaded with the headers the reservation signed, then named in the signed payload). The `Idempotency-Key` is the queue row's and the recipient's (`o<row>-<16 hex of the address>`); the relay's `idempotency_key_reused` (an earlier try got through) counts as sent. `address_unknown`, `address_closed` and the shape refusals are refused for that recipient; everything else retries, a gateway's 5xx with no code as "may have gone".
- **Email from the desk's address:** members' addresses (the Courier domain) get it sealed as `ma`, keeping its subject and its place in the email conversation (`rfcMessageId`, `inReplyTo`, `references`), **never through `courier-send`** (the plan §2.10). Everyone else gets the `MimeWriter` bytes through the `CourierSend` seam, keyed by the queue row (`s<row>`), so a try whose answer was lost isn't sent twice.
- **The seam:** `CourierSend` (`available()`, `submit(Submission)`). `PlatformCourierSend` is the format of section 9; the test kit's fake relay serves it. **The queue's senders exist only while they can send:** `ma` with an address and keys; the desk's email also needs `sending.available` from the platform. Until then `courier.send` answers `unavailable {reason: medium}` before doing anything, as the store's queue does for any medium with no transport.

### 6. Delivery notes
The door seals notes to the desk in `mail` envelopes (section 9). `delivery` sets the recipient's status on the member's sent message (`delivered`, `bounced`, `complained`, `deferred`, `refused` for `suppressed`); a message every recipient of which bounced, complained or was refused is `failed`. `removed` (the door dropped a message with a virus) is counted in `courier.address`'s `relay.removedByDoor`; how it's shown is the Designer's (question 3).

### 7. The picker (design §8.3, the plan §1.5)
`courier.compose.pick {channelId} | {to}`: the medium and account of the last message received from the other party in the channel (in a group, from any of them) when a transport can send on it now and it reaches everyone; else the channel's preferred medium; else `ma` when everyone has a member's address; else none, and the member picks. The reason comes back (`last_inbound` with its medium and time, `preferred`, `all_members`, `choose`, `key_changed`) for the composer's line ("By email · her last message was an email, 2 h ago"), with every account as an option (`sendable`, `reaches`, `blocked: key_changed`).

### 8. Switches
In Courier's switch file, as every transport: `ma-off` (members' messages both ways; their envelopes wait in the relay), `ma-send-off`; `door-off` (the desk's address both ways), `door-send-off`; `email-off` covers the door too; `send-off` and `off` as for all of Courier.

### 9. Proposed for contract 0.4.1 (Platform, CR-2.3 and CR-2.4)
- **`GET /v1/agent/courier-addresses` adds** `doorKeys: [{key}]` (the door's Ed25519 public keys, base64url; a desk verifies `mail` payloads with these only, and while there are none, mail waits) and `sending: {available}` (whether `courier-send` takes this desk's mail now).
- **The door's `mail` payload** (draft-courier-envelope §9) **adds** `verdicts.dmarcDomain` (the domain SES checked DMARC for, the header From's; without it a pass is never shown as confirmed, Security CP-L2) and a third form, `note`: `{type: delivery, rfcMessageId, recipient, status: delivered | bounced | complained | deferred | suppressed}` or `{type: removed, reason}`. Exactly one of `raw`, `drop`, `note`. `verdicts.spam` and `virus` as booleans (SES's `FAIL` is read as true).
- **`POST /v1/agent/courier/send`** `{from, to: [1..50 addresses], rfcMessageId, raw: base64url}` with an `Idempotency-Key` (kept 5 days, past the queue's 4-day retries) → `202 {id, accepted: [address], refused: [{address, code}]}`. Refusals: `400 from_mismatch` (From isn't the desk's address), `409 no_address`, `413 too_large` (25 MB), `429 rate_limited` (the tier's caps, D3), `403 send_off` (the per-desk switch), `503 no_provider`, `422 idempotency_key_reused` (the same key with other bytes: a retry writes its Date again; the desk counts it as sent). A member's address in `to` is refused per recipient (`member_address`). Up to 4 MiB inline (`raw`, 0.4.1's cap: a Lambda request holds about 6 MB); larger, `POST /v1/agent/courier/uploads {bytes}` → a presigned `PUT` of exactly those bytes (only its length signed), then `{upload: uploadId}`, up to 25 MiB (built Nov 15: `PlatformCourierSend` through the relay's drop uploader, `upload_missing` made again once; `PlatformCourierSendTest`, a 5 MB message).
- **The drop's signed headers** are sent as given (staging signs `Content-Length` and `x-amz-tagging`); a `Content-Length` that isn't the body's is refused before anything is sent. Drop links must be `https` with no user info.
- **A door key is served in `doorKeys` before the door signs anything with it** (a desk waits an hour of misses, then refuses).
- **For Platform (Security CT-I4):** `collect` issues and counts a link for every drop at every listing, so one drop envelope that legitimately waits spends the desk's daily links at every drain; check the per-drop cap first and charge only a link actually issued, or issue links on demand.

### 10. Schema
`courier.db` version 4: `ma_pins`, `ma_held`, `relay_seen` (above), after Wave 3.2's version 3. Nothing in versions 1 to 3 changes.

## Security's read
Stand-in read (`~/work/maia-mode/courier-transports/security.md`, Nov 14): one High, three Mediums, two Lows, five Infos, all fixed but CT-I4 (Platform's, in section 9): CT-H1 (an envelope with a drop of signed length -1 threw out of the drain and stopped every later one), CT-M1 (a `from` off the Courier domain waited for good and spent a lookup each drain), CT-M2 (an invite with a pin checked nothing: `rotated` was assumed, and a link replaced a verified pin unseen), CT-M3 (the drain could hold about 176 MiB in deskd's 208 MiB heap), CT-L1 (held caps by count only), CT-L2 (a door key rotation refused mail after a second), CT-I1 (loopback `http` drop links allowed in production), CT-I2, CT-I3, CT-I5. The fixes were confirmed, with two more found and fixed: CT-L3 (a door-key miss re-read the addresses and drained about once a second; now at most every 5 minutes) and CT-I6 (a 401 or 403 with no code, this desk's own credentials or clock, refused a stranger's first message; now only the directory's answers about the address are lasting).

## The proof
- **The fake relay** (courier-testkit, contract 0.4.0 plus section 9): collecting once through lost acks and duplicated pages; a sleeping desk never pushed and caught up at start; the push; the door's mail inline and through a drop with its verdicts; mail waiting without the door's key and never taken from another signer; forged senders before and after a pin, a payload for someone else, a `mail` payload in an `ma` envelope, an unknown address, garbage; a replay after the member deleted the message and a 32-day-old message; a changed key holding and blocking until the number is checked; a directory that swaps keys; sending with a retry, through the drop, to a group, to a closed address; desk-address email through `courier-send` with a lost answer, a member's copy sealed, a bounce and a door's removal note; no provider, no email sender; `ma-off`. 10,000 sealed messages drained in about 7 seconds (the bar: 2 minutes). The soak harness: 2,000 messages over 30 s through the relay down, lost acks, duplicates, the desk stopped and deskd killed: 0 lost, 0 doubled.
- **The real staging relay:** deskd's own code on this machine as two self-hosted test desks on two staging test accounts (`services/deskd/e2e/courier-relay-proof.mjs`, `RelayStagingIT`), 22 of 22: keys published, addresses read, the directory's fingerprint agreeing, a message each way, pinning on first contact into Requests, the same safety number on both sides, an invite matching, a 125 KB message through a staging drop, a message delivered twice stored once, a replay after deletion refused, a forged sender refused, an identity change held and blocking until the number was checked, then released and sent.

## Consequences
- The desk's address receives mail and members' messages the moment Platform's door and the desks' images carry it; sending from the address waits only on `courier-send`.
- A first contact without an invite trusts the platform's directory; everything after is pinned.
- A member's held messages are plaintext in `courier.db`, as every message is.
- **Rejected:** acking before storing (a crash loses mail); a drain per kind (two acks, two counts); trusting the directory on every message (the platform could swap a key unseen); dropping a message under a changed key (the contact's real new key would lose it); sending a member's address through `courier-send` (the platform would read a message between members).

## Questions, each with the default taken
1. **The proposed 0.4.1 additions (Platform):** section 9. *Default:* as proposed; the seam takes whatever 0.4.1 settles on.
2. **A message under a changed key** is held, not shown. *Default:* held, with the count on the contact (`courier.contact.keys`' `held`), released on the member's check; Signal shows a "safety number changed" line and the message. (Product Designer)
3. **The door's `removed` note** is counted only. *Default:* counted in `courier.address`; the Designer says whether the thread shows "A message from {sender} was removed because it carried a virus". (Product Designer)
4. **Lookups past the platform's caps** (300 an hour) leave a stranger's first message waiting. *Default:* waits for the next drain; a desk flooded by many new senders catches up within hours. (Platform)
5. **Held messages' caps** (200 an address, 2,000 a desk, 30 days). *Default:* as built.
