# 0053. Open tiles: an app enlarged on its desk is the live app there

**Status:** Accepted (the owner's ask, 2026-11-11; the calls are the UI/UX Engineer's)
**Date:** 2026-11-11
**Amends:** [0062](0062-desk-surface-etched-name-and-cleanup.md) (detail levels topped out at `large`), [0064](0064-desk-stacking-hidden-items-and-etch.md) (minimum sizes and stacking are unchanged)

## Context
The owner, on 2026-11-11: "When I expand the apps enough, they are supposed to actually be viewable and usable right there on my desk like when they are opened into models. If we need to fix the aspect ratio for some apps then we need to do that but still... All apps should be able to be 'opened' by expansion."

A tile's level was `detailLevel(w, h)`: `icon | small | wide | large`, topping out at `large` (4 x 4 cells), all drawn by Konva. Only a window or the carousel showed the live app. The carousel (`carousel/CarouselLayer.tsx`) already docks a live app to a desk's rectangle as DOM over the canvas, follows the camera with one transform while it moves, lays out again at rest, and keeps the one-live-picture rule (`shownDesk`). Constraints: the canvas can't draw over DOM; the Desk app's initial JS was 246.1 of 250 KB; the Browser streams, so only what's looked at may be live.

## Decision
1. **A new top level, `open`, above `large`.** A tile is `open` when it's at least 6 cells wide and 5 tall (`span(6)` x `span(5)`, 536 x 444 units). An app may need more: `OPEN_MIN` in `canvas/layout.ts`, beside the level it decides (the first load's, and pure: the registry holds the windows, so it can't be read from there). Only the Browser does, at 7 x 5 (628 x 444), for the width of its bar and picture. Every window's minimum is the same 360 x 280 px (`windowStore` `MIN_W`, `MIN_H`), and no window's CSS needs more than the live size below, so the rest take the default. `CELLS.open` is 6 x 5; `SMALLER.open` is `large`, so `fittedLevel` and the phone grid draw an open tile as `large`.
2. **No snapping for a ratio.** The member's size is kept. The Browser's picture fits the desk's stream inside the tile with bars in the window's own background (`.br-view`), exactly as its window does when sized off the picture's shape (its `<video>` is `object-fit: contain`; the window never snaps). Every other app reflows to the tile as a window does when resized.
3. **Live where, and how many** (`opentile/model.ts` `liveTiles`). Only on the shown desk (`shownDesk`, the carousel's one-live-picture rule), not in carousel mode, at rest, when the tile is at least 480 x 320 CSS px on screen (the carousel's 480, with a height that keeps the head and a few lines of the app), partly on screen, and with nothing above it in the desk's stack over it (the canvas can't draw over the DOM; a covered tile stays a drawing, stacked as the canvas stacks it). At most 4 at once, the largest on screen first; the rest draw the `large` widget. During a pan or zoom the layer follows with one transform; at rest it's laid out again, never remounted for either (`canvas/useCameraRest.ts`, now shared with the carousel). A tile that stops being live at rest unmounts as a closed window does (the Browser asks `off`), as the carousel's stage does below its size, and mounts once when it's live again. Kept mounted and hidden instead, a Browser on the desk that's looked at would keep its stream `live` under `desk.attention` with nothing showing it.
4. **One place per app**: its window, else the carousel, else its open tile. With its window open the tile draws its widget and the note "Open in its window" in the widget's foot; closing the window brings it back live, and the keyboard to its tile. In carousel mode the carousel wins and the desk's tiles are drawn as before.
5. **Chrome.** A slim head (the app's glyph and its name as an `h2`, as a window's title bar but quieter) that moves the tile; a grip at every corner and edge that resizes it, free placement as every tile on a larger screen (ADR 0062: no cells to snap to there, clamped to the work area, down to `G.minApp`), the bottom-right one a 24 px target with a mark inside the round corner that shows while the pointer or the keyboard's ring is on the tile and strengthens under the pointer; and one button, "Open in its window" (the window's expand glyph, with that title and name, since a lone square reads as "maximize", the Designer). A press on the head brings the tile to the front. Pointer and wheel events inside the app are the app's: the canvas is under the DOM, so it never sees them, and a pinch over a tile is in `pinch.ts`'s own-pinch list, so it zooms nothing (inside the Browser's picture the page has it, as in a window). While a tile is live its Konva node is hidden and the frame's ground is opaque, so the canvas paints nothing under the app.
6. **Keys.** The app is inside the `ma-window` wrapper (`windows/LiveWindow.tsx`, shared with the carousel), and the office's keys leave everything in a tile alone, so the canvas's single keys never fire there. The tile's one stop in the desk's keyboard layer is its proxy, as any app's (arrows, `m`, `r`, `.` as everywhere); the frame draws the proxy's ring, which would otherwise be under it. Tab, Enter or Space on it go into the app. Esc goes to the app first; one it doesn't use (and Shift+Esc always) returns to the proxy, and never closes a window or leaves the desk; plain Esc on the Browser's picture is the page's.
7. **Phones (under 600 px)** keep the grid; `open` doesn't apply. A desk saved with open tiles shows them as `large` there.
8. **Kill switch only, shipped on.** client-config `openTiles: { enabled: false }`: nothing goes past `large` (`setOpenLevel`) and the layer's chunk never loads. Absent is on. Platform's member function doesn't carry the key yet (it has no generic pass-through), so it needs a settings row and a line in `clientConfig` there, as `simpleView` and `browserLinks` do.
9. **Bundle.** Only the level and the way in are in the first load (`canvas/layout.ts`, `opentile/door.ts`, the widget's note, one line in `useKeyboard`, one in `pinch.ts`, one rule in `openItem`): 0.34 KB, 246.3 to 246.7 KB on `main` at `d6d3a83d`. Everything else, the live rules and the keys included, is the layer's own lazy chunk (`OpenTileLayer`, 3.1 KB), loaded when a tile first reaches `open` on the shown desk or on a load with one.
10. **State.** The tile and the window hold the same component, so what it shows (the Notebook's page, the Browser's tab) carries over between them, as between a window and the carousel.
11. **Apps at a tile's width answer to their own width** (the Designer's review): the Notebook (`container: ma-nb`) steps its notebooks and pages column aside behind a toggle in its head under 640 px on a larger screen, so the page takes the width and its toolbar fits, and Files (`container: files`) keeps its actions on one row that scrolls, so a selection's buttons never move the list under a double click. A narrow window gets the same. The Notebook's Zen ground follows the owner's slate rule (`#4B5162`, sidebar `#444A5A`).

A press on an open tile that isn't live (too small on screen, over the cap) opens its window as before. From the overview, a press on a tile at `open` brings its desk into focus and opens no window: the tile is live there if it's big enough on that screen, and a press on it there opens its window if it isn't.

## Consequences
- Enlarging a tile is opening its app: the desk itself becomes the workspace, several apps side by side, with no window over the office.
- At 1280 x 800 two open tiles fit side by side live; a third, or a desk zoomed out under 480 px a tile, shows the widget.
- The live app is DOM over the canvas, so anything the canvas draws above it in the stack keeps it from being live; the member brings it to the front with a press, as any app.
- Commons draws its board under its own panels, 96 % opaque, so at a narrow width (a tile, or its window as narrow) the board shows faintly through them. That's its window's look, the Guild Engineer's; nothing of the canvas is under a live tile.
- Tests: `opentile/model.test.ts`, the open cases in `layout.test.ts`; `opentiles.shell.spec.ts`.
