# 0045. A desk nobody looks at encodes nothing, and its Chromium freezes

**Status:** Accepted (the owner, task 46, 2026-11-07). `deskd`'s part is built on `sub/desk-idle-freeze`, in no release and on no desk. The image's part, the `desk-units` verbs, is the System Engineer's: `docs/sub-status/desk-idle-freeze.md`. *(Status updated 2026-11-09 from the code on main: `sub/desk-idle-freeze` is merged, and the freeze, with `deskd` 2026.11.08-8a14353, is baked into image 0.3.0: `image/RELEASES.md`.)*
**Date:** 2026-11-07
**Amends:** design §6.1 and §11.5, which said "CDP page lifecycle `frozen`"; `desk-runtime.md` §8.3 (two verbs) and §8.4 (what `warm` and `off` leave running).

## Context
Task 46, the owner: "find a way to get 6 on a desk [...] this is more important than any other problem we need to solve". Lever 2: a desk nobody is looking at doesn't encode at all, and when it also isn't playing sound or working, its Chromium freezes. Waking is instant and never loses a tab or a sign-in. The owner's binding rule stands: a desk's browser session, history and cookies are never lost or cleared (ADR 0035).

Shared-host test 3 found a viewed desk that's idle costs about 36% of a core, 25 of it `desk-stream`. A member has two desks and looks at one, so half the desks on a host are encoding for nobody, and every desk's Chromium runs its pages whether anyone looks or not.

What each stream state runs today, from the code: `live` with a viewer captures at 60 fps and encodes. `warm`, and `live` with no viewer, leave `desk-stream` up with its GStreamer pipeline paused, where live sources produce nothing, so nothing is captured or encoded: about 0 CPU and 32 to 57 MB. `off` stops it. Chromium runs the same in all three. So the encoder costs nothing already whenever no session gets media. The waste is a session that keeps `live` for a desk nobody looks at, and Chromium.

## Decision
1. **Whether anyone looks is the client's to say:** `desk.attention.set {seen}` per session (the UI/UX Engineer's contract, adopted as given). The desk is looked at if any session says so. A session that has never said counts as looking while it wants `warm` or `live`, so clients from before this work unchanged.
2. **Nobody looking for 5 s cuts every session's media,** and keeps what each wants. `desk-rtc` pauses `desk-stream`, so nothing is captured or encoded, and `desk-stream` stays up, so a look is `warm` to `live`: 74 to 98 ms p50 to a first frame, against 287 to 291 ms from a stopped encoder on `c7g.large` (`docs/benchmarks/2026-11-real-stack.md`). Both are inside B14's 1.5 s. Stopping `desk-stream` too would save 32 to 57 MB a desk for about 200 ms more on every look, so it isn't done. **So `warm` keeps its meaning,** the paused pipeline, and an unseen `live` costs what `warm` does.
3. **Chromium freezes with systemd's cgroup freezer,** `systemctl freeze desk-browser`, through two new `units.sock` verbs, `freeze` and `thaw`. It holds every process of the unit in one step, with no change of state that anything in Chromium or its pages can see, and it costs nothing while frozen. CDP's `Page.setWebLifecycleState frozen` was the design's choice, and is rejected: it reaches only pages deskd has attached to, one at a time, leaves the browser, GPU, network and utility processes running, misses a tab opened meanwhile, and fires the page's `freeze` and `resume` events, so every page sees it. **Amended 2026-11-07 (measured on the image):** on systemd 257 a unit that systemd froze can't be stopped, and a reboot left Chromium crashed, so `desk-units` writes the cgroup's `cgroup.freeze` directly, and `systemctl freeze` is not used. systemd's `FreezerState` therefore always reads "running": anything checking the frozen state reads `cgroup.events` (`frozen 1`) or asks `desk-units`, and `deskd` itself only reads `desk-units`' answers. `desk-units` refuses with `inactive`, `busy download` or `busy network` (over 16 KiB in 0.5 s). A stop of `desk-browser` first runs a quit from `desk-browser-thaw` and can take about 80 s, so `desk-units` waits 90 s and `deskd` allows 95 s for that one verb and unit (`UnitsClient.STOP_BROWSER_TIMEOUT`, and 100 s for a hold's wait in `DataModule.Timing`); freeze, thaw and start keep 45 s. `deskd` still thaws for up to 2 s before it stops. Measured thaw to first frame: p50 68 to 73 ms on `c7g.xlarge` and 86 ms on `t4g.medium`, inside B13's 300 ms.
4. **When:** nobody has viewed the Browser for 60 s, which is looking at the desk with a Browser window open, so a desk looked at only for its Notebook freezes too, as design §6.1 had it; nothing has worked Chromium for 60 s, or works it now; no hold, restart, start or stop of `desk-browser` is under way or owed, and the age gate is open; Chromium is up on its port; and no member tab is loading, as far as deskd has heard. At the moment of the freeze, `desk-units` declines with `busy` while a download is being written or Chromium is moving more than about 64 KB over the network in 2 s, and deskd takes any `busy`, whatever its reason, as a clear no. Working is deskd's own calls that reach Chromium, input on the screen from `desk-rtc`, a loading tab, a download and the network. **Sound isn't** (decided with the UI/UX Engineer, 2026-11-07): media goes only to a desk somebody views, so after 60 s with nobody viewing, sound is heard by nobody. It pauses with Chromium and carries on after the thaw, and a call or a stream in a page is held off by `busy network`. The freeze's grace is longer than the stream's, so a member flipping between desks doesn't freeze and thaw them.
5. **Thaw first, always, and never count on systemd for it.** systemd 257 thaws nothing for a job: a start or stop of a frozen unit fails (`JOB_FROZEN`), and a start of a running frozen unit answers ok and leaves it frozen, whatever `systemctl(1)` says. So unless Chromium is known to be running, deskd has no connection to it and makes none. Every call that would reach it, a `live`, and every start or stop of `desk-browser`, waits for deskd's own thaw, and only a thaw that answers `ok` ends a freeze. Input and a look ask for one without waiting. deskd thaws before it stops, as it stops before `desk-browser` on the way down, and `desk-units`' `stop`, `desk-browser-close` and a step ordered before `desk-browser`'s stop at shutdown thaw too, for a deskd that didn't.
6. **On any doubt, thaw.** A freeze that ends in anything but `ok` or a clear no is followed at once by a thaw, which is tried again until it works, and calls get `unavailable` meanwhile. Every deskd thaws once as it starts, before anything touches Chromium. A file beside `units.sock`, `browser-frozen`, says an earlier one may have left Chromium frozen, which changes only what it reports meanwhile, as a marker that couldn't be written would leave no sign.
7. **What's said of it:** `/health` has `browser.freeze` and `stream.seen`, the heartbeat `health.checks.browser: "frozen"` while frozen, and the topic `desk.attention` `{seen, frozen}`. Nothing of why, or of what the member has open.
8. **No still from deskd.** A frozen desk's screen doesn't change, so the client's last decoded frame is its still. A CDP screenshot on the way into a freeze is the fallback if the office needs one for a desk it hasn't shown since it opened.

## Consequences
- An unseen desk costs no encoding, and a frozen one no Chromium CPU. Its memory stays, and can go to zram under pressure, as nothing touches it.
- The profile is untouched by any of this, so ADR 0035 holds: no tab, cookie, history entry or sign-in is closed, cleared or reloaded. A page sees time jump on after a thaw, as after a laptop's sleep, and its server may have dropped a connection meanwhile, which pages reconnect.
- Waking a frozen desk adds a thaw, a Python process and `systemctl`, before Chromium answers. The picture comes back at once from the frame on screen. Not yet measured on a desk.
- A desk whose image doesn't know the verbs gets the encoder half only, and `/health` says `unsupported`.
- With the Desk app as it is, closing the Browser window lets Chromium freeze 60 s later, as design §6.1 meant. An upload left running then stalls on an image without the `network` check, and goes on with it.
- Sound playing on a desk nobody looks at stops reaching the member after 5 s, with the picture, as `desk-rtc` sends them together, and pauses with Chromium at the freeze. To keep hearing a desk, the office keeps it `seen` (ADR 0038's rule, moved to the office).
- A desk being exported is unseen, as the export page looks at no desk, so it freezes. `data.parts.list`, `data.manifest`, `data.read` and the free-byte budget go to `desk-data` and never to Chromium, so an export reads on while it's frozen. `data.browser.cookies` thaws it first, and `data.browser.hold` and `release` thaw it before their stop and start.
- A load deskd didn't hear, because it wasn't connected when it began, doesn't hold the freeze off. Nothing without a connection starts one but a page by itself, and a frozen load carries on after the thaw.
